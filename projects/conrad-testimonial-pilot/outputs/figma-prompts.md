# Design Direction Summary

## Selected Concept

CON-03: Benefit Validation Experience is the approved selected concept. The design exploration should help guests understand why Conrad Complete is credible, premium, and relevant by turning package inclusions into an evidence-backed decision experience.

CON-01: Editorial Feature Experience and CON-02: Guided Package Fit Experience are supporting influences only. Editorial recognition should support benefit validation, and fit guidance should preserve honest right-guest nuance without becoming a separate concept.

## Primary Exploration Direction

Benefit Evidence Matrix on the Conrad Complete Package Surface.

The primary exploration should organize Conrad Complete inclusions into decision-ready proof units that explain what is included, why it matters, what evidence supports it, and what terms or fit considerations keep the package story accurate.

## Secondary Exploration Direction

Editorial Proof Bridge supporting the Benefit Evidence Matrix, with Fit Guidance Panel and Inclusion Detail Disclosure as subordinate support layers.

The secondary exploration should show how governed editorial recognition can strengthen trust near Club 66 and dining without becoming a generic testimonial, standalone hero, endorsement badge, ranking, or award claim.

## Primary Placement

Conrad Complete Package Surface, using the observed Summer Offers Conrad Complete module as the practical working surface until the canonical dedicated package surface is confirmed.

## Secondary Placement

Summer Offers / Offer Detail Surface, with the Club 66 Section as a supporting proof extension rather than a competing primary surface.

## Core Experience Pattern

Benefit Evidence Matrix.

## Supporting Patterns

- Governed editorial recognition moment.
- Lightweight fit guidance.
- Inclusion detail support.
- Source-aware proof bridge for Club 66 and dining.
- Terms-aware package qualification.
- Restrained booking confidence handoff.

## Existing Component Reuse Requirement

`component-inventory.md` is unavailable, so component reuse could not be verified against a documented design-system inventory. Reuse guidance is therefore based only on `outputs/selected-concept.md`, `outputs/design-review.md`, and documented current content or observed patterns.

Do not invent named reusable components. Use generic preservation and adaptation guidance for existing package content, offer modules, inclusion lists, menu links, terms areas, detail disclosures, trust callouts, and the current `BOOK NOW` handoff.

## Input Limitations

- Screenshots and mobile captures are unavailable, so visual hierarchy, density, and responsive behavior must be explored rather than assumed.
- The dedicated Conrad Complete URL was not directly reviewable, so the observed Summer Offers Conrad Complete module remains the practical working surface until the canonical package surface is confirmed.
- Editorial quote usage, attribution, logo use, excerpt rights, and paraphrase rules are unavailable. Press recognition must remain governed and placeholder-safe.
- Booking-engine states are only partially reviewed. Do not redesign the booking flow.

## Benefit Validation Experience Requirements

These requirements apply because the selected concept is CON-03: Benefit Validation Experience and the supporting strategy includes confidence building, value communication, proof-based evaluation, and package differentiation.

For all prompts:

- Preserve benefit-level proof relationships.
- Place validation adjacent to the specific benefit it supports.
- Treat editorial validation as supporting evidence for a benefit, not as a separate content destination.
- Prefer benefit validation over testimonial aggregation.
- Avoid testimonial carousels, review walls, press logo collections, and generic quote galleries unless a later review explicitly requires them.
- Use the hierarchy: What's Included, Why It Matters, Validation Evidence, Terms / Constraints.
- Explore comparison moments as decision support, such as Standard Room vs Package, Included vs Purchased Separately, General Access vs Included Access, and Typical Stay vs Package Stay.
- Treat this concept as an inserted module within an existing Conrad Complete experience, not a standalone marketing landing page.
- Preserve upstream content, downstream CTAs, surrounding page structure, and the existing `BOOK NOW` CTA.
- If editorial validation is approved and available, place it inside the benefit card, row, or section it supports.
- Do not create a dedicated Editorial Recognition section unless a later Design Review explicitly recommends one.
