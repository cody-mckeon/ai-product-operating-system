## Objective

Introduce Savor Society on the existing Dining page so guests understand that it is a year-round, cross-venue culinary and cocktail collective.

The enhancement must:

- Increase culinary-programming awareness.
- Connect Savor Society to recognizable Dining venues.
- Preserve Dining as the primary place to discover and reserve restaurants and lounges.
- Provide one clear path to the canonical Savor Society page.
- Keep restaurant, event, signup, and hotel-booking actions distinct.

## Experience Intent

The module is an editorial orientation experience, not an advertisement or event listing.

It answers three guest questions:

1. What is Savor Society?
2. How does it relate to Dining venues?
3. Where can I explore its programming?

The module must feel warm, composed, premium, editorial, and operationally clear. It should orient before asking for action and use restraint rather than urgency or promotional intensity.

## Scope

### In Scope

- One inserted Savor Society orientation section on the existing Dining page.
- One approved editorial image.
- Collective definition and program-type labels.
- Three governed participating-venue examples.
- Text links from venue names to their existing canonical venue-detail pages.
- One primary program-discovery CTA linking to the existing Savor Society page.
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile treatment.
- Accessible semantic structure, links, focus states, image treatment, and reading order.

### Out Of Scope

- Redesigning the Dining page.
- Replacing or changing the Dining hero.
- Changing the Dining introduction or filters.
- Changing Dining categories, restaurant cards, or restaurant detail pages.
- Changing restaurant reservation destinations or booking behavior.
- Changing global navigation or the Dining submenu.
- Creating a standalone Savor Society landing page.
- Duplicating the Savor Society program inventory or creating an event calendar.
- Adding event-reservation, list-signup, hotel-booking, offer, package, or loyalty CTAs to this module.
- Adding carousels, tabs, accordions, modal flows, personalization, or new navigation behavior.
- Introducing new event, venue, membership, availability, pricing, or priority-access claims.

## Placement

Place the module on the existing Dining page:

**After the Dining introduction and core discovery controls, before the first full venue category.**

The surrounding sequence must remain:

1. Existing Dining hero and promotional content.
2. Existing Dining discovery controls.
3. Existing Dining introduction.
4. New Savor Society orientation module.
5. Existing first venue category and the remainder of restaurant discovery.

Do not place the module:

- Inside the hero or promotional carousel.
- Inside the filter controls.
- As a replacement for a Dining category.
- Between every venue category.
- At the bottom of the full venue inventory.
- In global navigation as part of this scope.

The transition from the module into the first venue category must remain visually obvious so guests can continue restaurant discovery without interruption.

## Existing Dining Experience To Preserve

The following must remain intact and function as they do today:

- Global header and navigation.
- Dining hero and promotional imagery.
- Dining introduction.
- Date/time, category, and cuisine filters.
- Filtered and no-results states.
- Fine Dining, Casual Dining, Famous Foods, Bars & Lounges, and Quick Eats hierarchy.
- Venue summaries and venue-detail links.
- Restaurant reservation links and destinations.
- Hotel booking area.
- Breadcrumbs and footer.
- Existing responsive page behavior outside the inserted module.

## Approved Module Anatomy

Maintain this information order:

1. Functional eyebrow label.
2. Section heading.
3. Concise collective definition.
4. Program-type taxonomy.
5. Short divider or equivalent existing visual convention.
6. Participating-venue label.
7. Three venue examples with short relationship descriptors.
8. Scope/freshness disclaimer.
9. One program-discovery CTA.
10. One approved editorial image.

The module must not become a second hero, an event grid, or a second venue directory.

## Content Rules

- Describe Savor Society as a collective, never as a restaurant, venue, dining category, event calendar, offer, package, or loyalty program.
- Keep the language stable and collective-level; do not add current-event dates, prices, availability, or talent claims.
- Venue relationships must be verified before publication.
- Do not imply that listed venues participate continuously.
- Do not imply that the three examples are the complete roster.
- Do not add “exclusive,” “limited,” “priority,” “members-only,” or urgency language.
- Do not include press quotes, publication marks, awards, testimonials, or partner logos.
- Preserve the accent and capitalization in `ALLĒ LOUNGE ON 66`.

## Visual Direction

Follow the latest concept screenshot without converting the module into a fixed-pixel recreation.

### Composition

- Use an editorial two-part composition on wider screens: content and approved imagery.
- Keep the content hierarchy dominant enough to explain the collective before the CTA.
- Maintain generous but purposeful spacing.
- Keep the module subordinate to the Dining hero and page title.
- Use one focal image only.
- Do not add card grids, floating badges, decorative arrows, image carousels, or secondary promotional panels.

### Brand Expression

- Warm, premium, welcoming, and composed.
- Editorial rather than campaign-led.
- Confident without shouting.
- Commercially clear without pressure.
- Integrated with the existing Dining page rather than visually imported from a standalone landing page.

### Avoid

- Neon, glow, casino styling, or decorative gradients.
- Generic SaaS card layouts.
- Dark luxury treatment that reduces warmth or readability.
- Oversized display typography that competes with the Dining hero.
- Excessive letter spacing or all-caps body copy.
- Multiple equal-weight focal points.
- Exact colors, fonts, sizes, spacing, or breakpoints that are not drawn from the existing RWLV implementation.

## Link And Conversion Behavior

### Savor Society CTA

- Opens the canonical Savor Society page.
- Uses the existing RWLV internal-link behavior unless the platform requires otherwise.
- Must not open a restaurant reservation, event reservation, signup form, or hotel-booking flow.

### Venue Links

- Venue names link to their existing canonical venue-detail pages.
- Use existing internal text-link conventions.
- Do not add separate “Learn More” or “Reserve” buttons inside the module.
- Do not link venue descriptors unless required by the existing link pattern.

### Reservation Continuity

- Existing restaurant reservation actions elsewhere on Dining remain unchanged.
- Event reservations remain on the canonical Savor Society or event experience.
- List signup remains on the Savor Society journey.
- Hotel booking remains separate from this module.

## Responsive Behavior

### Desktop And Wide Tablet

- Preserve the approved content-and-image editorial relationship.
- Keep venue evidence subordinate to the heading and definition.
- Maintain clear separation between the CTA and nearby restaurant-reservation actions.
- Match the existing Dining page container and spacing system.

### Mobile And Narrow Tablet

Use this sequential reading order:

1. Eyebrow.
2. Heading.
3. Introduction.
4. Program types.
5. Image.
6. Participating-venue examples.
7. Disclaimer.
8. Primary CTA.
9. Immediate continuation into the first venue category.

Mobile requirements:

- Keep the heading compact and readable.
- Keep body copy and descriptors concise.
- Ensure program-type labels wrap cleanly and remain non-interactive.
- Avoid a full-screen image treatment that delays restaurant results.
- Preserve the image subject and meaningful setting when cropped.
- Keep venue links easy to distinguish and activate.
- Avoid multiple stacked button treatments.
- Ensure the next Dining category is visually recognizable after the module.

## Accessibility Requirements

- Use a semantic section within the existing page hierarchy.
- Use a heading level that follows the existing Dining document outline; do not introduce another page-level heading.
- Treat the eyebrow and participating-venue label as labels, not headings unless the existing semantic pattern requires otherwise.
- Use a real list or equivalent accessible grouping for venue examples.
- Make each linked venue name understandable outside surrounding visual styling.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus for venue links and the Savor Society CTA.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast for the gold eyebrow, small labels, body copy, disclaimer, link underlines, and button outline.
- Do not rely on color, bullets, underline, or imagery alone to communicate the relationship.
- Mark decorative separators so they are ignored by assistive technology.
- Provide meaningful alt text for the approved image; use empty alt text only if the image is formally designated decorative.
- Preserve logical keyboard and screen-reader order across responsive states.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; no motion is required for this module.

## States And Fallbacks

### Confirmed Standard State

Show the approved module with governed copy, three confirmed venue examples, valid links, one CTA, and an approved image.

### Venue Relationship Unconfirmed

Do not show the affected venue example. Do not replace it with a guessed venue. Escalate if the remaining evidence no longer communicates the cross-venue relationship adequately.

### Image Unavailable Or Unapproved

Do not ship the prototype or generic stock. Use only an approved fallback authorized by RWLV.

### Savor Society Destination Unavailable

Do not publish a broken CTA. Resolve the destination before release.

### Venue Destination Unavailable

Do not publish a broken venue link. Confirm the canonical destination or remove the link treatment until resolved, subject to RWLV approval.

### No Current Marquee Event

No change is required. The module intentionally uses stable collective-level orientation and does not depend on a current event.

- The approved program-type labels are present and non-interactive.

- `EXPLORE SAVOR SOCIETY` is the only button-style CTA.
- The CTA links to the canonical Savor Society page.

## Change Control

Vendor changes that require RWLV approval before implementation:

- Moving the module from the approved placement.
- Changing the information order.
- Changing the approved copy or taxonomy.
- Adding, removing, or replacing venue examples.
- Adding buttons, event actions, signup actions, filters, tabs, cards, or animation.
- Changing the Savor Society or venue destinations.
- Using a different image subject or unapproved asset.
- Changing the module into a hero, banner, event listing, or venue category.
- Modifying existing Dining components or reservation behavior.

When a requirement cannot be met with the existing RWLV component system, document the gap and return it for design review. Do not create a new pattern or alter the approved concept without authorization.
