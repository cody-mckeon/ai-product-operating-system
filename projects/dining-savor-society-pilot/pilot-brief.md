# Pilot Overview

This pilot will evaluate how Savor Society should be represented on the Resorts World Las Vegas Dining page. The work will establish the inputs needed to explore a connection between restaurant discovery and relevant culinary programming while preserving the Dining page's primary role.

# Business Context

Savor Society is a year-round culinary and cocktail collective at Resorts World Las Vegas. Its programming includes chef collaborations, mixology takeovers, recurring dining experiences, and special food-and-beverage events. The current Savor Society page is located under Experiences, while the Dining page serves guests discovering dining options at the resort.

# Stakeholder Request

Include Savor Society on the Dining page.

# Problem Statement

Determine how the Dining page can promote Savor Society in a way that strengthens dining discovery, supports awareness of culinary programming, and connects guests to relevant Savor Society experiences without disrupting restaurant discovery.

# Goals

- Clarify the role Savor Society should play within the Dining page experience.
- Support guest awareness of Savor Society's culinary and cocktail programming.
- Help guests move between restaurant discovery and relevant Savor Society experiences.
- Preserve the clarity and utility of the Dining page for guests primarily seeking restaurants.
- Establish a focused basis for evaluating the pilot.

# Success Criteria

- Guests can identify what Savor Society is and why it is relevant to dining at Resorts World Las Vegas.
- Guests interested in culinary programming can find and access the Savor Society experience from the Dining page.
- The inclusion of Savor Society does not materially impede restaurant discovery or access to core dining information.
- The relationship between the Dining page and the Savor Society page is clear and contextually relevant.
- Pilot evaluation can distinguish engagement with Savor Society from core restaurant-discovery behavior.

# In Scope

- The current Resorts World Las Vegas Dining page.
- The current Savor Society page under Experiences.
- Existing public context about Savor Society and its programming.
- The relationship between restaurant discovery and Savor Society discovery.
- Relevant Savor Society dining-event URLs identified during the pilot.
- Definition of pilot goals, assumptions, questions, and evaluation criteria.

# Out Of Scope

- Redesigning the Dining page or Savor Society page.
- Producing creative concepts, visual designs, or implementation specifications.
- Repositioning or renaming Savor Society.
- Changing restaurant listings, restaurant detail pages, booking flows, or event operations.
- Developing new Savor Society programming or content.
- Making final recommendations before pilot research and evaluation are complete.

# Open Questions

- What guest needs and tasks currently drive visits to the Dining page?
- How familiar are Dining page visitors with Savor Society?
- Which Savor Society experiences are most relevant to guests browsing dining options?
- How frequently does Savor Society programming change, and how is current programming maintained?
- Are individual Savor Society events represented by stable, public URLs?
- What analytics are currently available across the Dining and Savor Society pages?
- What baseline measures exist for restaurant discovery and Savor Society engagement?
- Which stakeholder teams own Dining page content, Savor Society content, and ongoing updates?
- Are there operational, editorial, legal, or technical constraints that the pilot must account for?

# Initial Hypotheses

- Dining page visitors are likely to find Savor Society relevant when its connection to food, cocktails, chefs, and dining experiences is immediately clear.
- Awareness of Savor Society among Dining page visitors is currently limited because its primary page is located under Experiences.
- Some guests browsing restaurants may also be interested in time-sensitive or recurring culinary programming.
- Relevance will vary based on the availability, recency, and specificity of active Savor Society programming.
- A successful connection between the pages will depend on preserving restaurant discovery as the Dining page's primary task.
- Existing analytics and event URL patterns may constrain how pilot performance can be measured.
