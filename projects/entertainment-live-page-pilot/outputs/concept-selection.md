# Executive Summary

## Primary Concept

CON-01: Resorts World Live Orientation Journey

## Supporting Concepts

* CON-02: Entertainment Map Of Experiences
* CON-04: Event Action Clarity Experience
* CON-06: Venue Pulse Experience

## Excluded Concepts

* CON-03: Choose Your Entertainment Path
* CON-05: Concert Weekend Planning Path

## Selection Rationale

CON-01 is the strongest direction for Design Review because it addresses the central pilot problem most directly: guests need to understand how Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk fit into the Entertainment page hierarchy while still being able to discover events and continue toward ticket actions.

CON-01 aligns tightly with stakeholder requests to add a Resorts World Live section after The Theatre section, represent The Stage at Zouk as the heading/context, improve page hierarchy, and preserve the known relationship that Resorts World Live should not be treated as a separate venue.

CON-02, CON-04, and CON-06 should inform the primary direction because they strengthen venue discovery, action clarity, and social context without replacing the main orientation problem. CON-03 and CON-05 are valuable but broader than the immediate Design Review need and carry higher risk around intent modeling and package availability.

# Concept Evaluation

## CON-01: Resorts World Live Orientation Journey

### Strengths

* Strongest alignment with the primary stakeholder need to clarify Resorts World Live positioning.
* Directly supports The Stage at Zouk representation without treating Resorts World Live as a separate venue.
* Addresses Entertainment page hierarchy, venue discovery, event discovery, and ticket conversion support.
* Lower content and operational complexity than a full ecosystem map or planning experience.
* Scales as a clear pattern for future venue/programming orientation moments.

### Weaknesses

* Narrower than concepts that reframe the full Entertainment ecosystem.
* Depends on approved relationship language, venue copy, and assets.
* Does not fully solve stay-package visibility or social integration on its own.

### Risks

* Poor taxonomy could make Resorts World Live appear like a separate venue.
* Too much explanatory content could slow guests who are ready to buy tickets.
* AEG and partner-governance constraints may affect where or how this appears.

### Recommendation Alignment

* Strong alignment: REC-01
* Moderate alignment: REC-02
* Limited alignment: REC-03, REC-04, REC-05

### User Impact

High. The concept directly reduces confusion about Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk and helps guests continue from orientation into event discovery.

### Business Impact

High. It improves visibility for Resorts World Live programming, supports The Stage at Zouk merchandising, and preserves ticket-path continuity.

### Feasibility Assessment

Medium-high. The concept requires content governance and approved assets but does not depend on package inventory, social integrations, or new business rules.

## CON-02: Entertainment Map Of Experiences

### Strengths

* Strong venue-discovery concept that can scale across future Entertainment enhancements.
* Helps guests understand the broader entertainment ecosystem.
* Provides a natural structure for navigation and anchor improvements.
* Can incorporate The Stage at Zouk as part of a larger venue/programming hierarchy.

### Weaknesses

* Broader than the immediate Resorts World Live clarification problem.
* May introduce too much choice if venue taxonomy is not settled.
* Risks diluting the specific focus on The Stage at Zouk.

### Risks

* Requires accurate content and ownership across multiple venues.
* May imply venue coverage that is not yet approved or maintained.
* Social content quality may vary across venues if included.

### Recommendation Alignment

* Strong alignment: REC-01, REC-04
* Moderate alignment: REC-02
* Limited alignment: REC-03, REC-05

### User Impact

Medium-high. It improves ecosystem comprehension and venue comparison, especially for guests who do not know where to start.

### Business Impact

Medium-high. It can increase venue consideration and make the Entertainment page more scalable, but it is less targeted to immediate ticket conversion than CON-01 or CON-04.

### Feasibility Assessment

Medium. Requires broader venue content governance and a confirmed venue inventory.

# Primary Concept Selection

## Concept Name

CON-01: Resorts World Live Orientation Journey

## Why Selected

CON-01 was selected because it most directly answers the highest-priority business problem: the Entertainment page needs to clarify how Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk fit into the page hierarchy while supporting discovery and ticket conversion.

It has the strongest alignment with REC-01, the strongest fit with the stakeholder requests, and the lowest dependency burden among the concepts that address the central problem. It can also absorb useful elements from CON-02, CON-04, and CON-06 without becoming a competing multi-directional experience.

## Evidence Alignment

The current-state and opportunity analysis show:

* Resorts World Live appears in global navigation and on its own Experiences page.
* The Stage at Zouk appears as an event-card venue label.
* The Entertainment page does not currently show a dedicated Resorts World Live body section after The Theatre section.
* The Entertainment page does not use `The Stage at Zouk` as an H2 section heading.
* The current on-page anchor set does not include Resorts World Live or The Stage at Zouk.
* FAQ currently appears before the Venues section.

## Business Alignment

CON-01 supports:

* Entertainment page hierarchy.
* Resorts World Live positioning.
* The Stage at Zouk representation.
* Venue discovery continuity.
* Ticket conversion support.
* Navigation and anchor clarity.

## Risks

* Requires precise taxonomy and relationship language.
* Requires approved content and assets for The Stage at Zouk and Resorts World Live.
* Needs AEG content/logo governance awareness.
* Must preserve ticket conversion paths and avoid over-explaining.

## Stakeholder Requests Addressed

* Add a new Resorts World Live section after The Theatre section.
* The Stage at Zouk should be the H2 heading for the Resorts World Live section.
* Move FAQ to the bottom of the page, as part of restoring venue-discovery continuity.
* Explore social media integrations for all entertainment venues, through supporting CON-06 influence.
* Add AEG logo placement and remove AEG content, as constraints to respect during Design Review rather than the main concept.

## Recommendation Themes Satisfied

* REC-01: Clarify Entertainment Hierarchy Around Resorts World Live.
* REC-02: Strengthen Event Discovery And Commercial Action Clarity.
* REC-04: Use Venue-Level Social Context To Support Discovery Confidence, as a supporting influence.
* REC-05: Clarify AEG Content, Logo, And Policy Governance, as a constraint.

## What Should Be Preserved During Design Review

* Resorts World Live must remain associated with The Stage at Zouk.
* The Stage at Zouk must be the user-facing venue context.
* The concept should improve hierarchy without burying event discovery.
* Ticket actions should remain easy to find and understand.
* Existing Theatre, Ayu Dayclub, and Zouk Nightclub discovery paths should remain coherent.
* FAQ should remain findable even if its placement changes.
* Social context should support confidence and not compete with ticket conversion.
* AEG logo/content handling should remain governed by partner and policy requirements.

# Unified Design Direction

### Experience Direction

Create a Resorts World Live orientation experience within the Entertainment page that presents The Stage at Zouk as the venue context, clarifies the relationship between programming and events, and connects guests to relevant ticket actions.

### User Problem Solved

Guests need to understand what Resorts World Live is, how it relates to The Stage at Zouk, and how to continue from that understanding into event discovery and ticket purchase.

### Core Pattern

Orientation Section

### Supporting Patterns

* Experience Directory cues from CON-02.
* Action Clarity cues from CON-04.
* Social Context cues from CON-06.

### Content Role

Content should explain the Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk relationship, identify the venue/programming context, support event discovery, and avoid presenting Resorts World Live as a separate venue.

### Trust Role

Trust comes from clear taxonomy, accurate venue representation, consistent actions, governed partner/logo handling, and optional social context that is official, brand-safe, and venue-specific.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests understand the Entertainment page structure more quickly, recognize The Stage at Zouk as the Resorts World Live context, and can proceed confidently into relevant ticket or event actions.

### Expected Business Outcome

The Entertainment page improves Resorts World Live discoverability, strengthens The Stage at Zouk merchandising, preserves ticket conversion paths, and creates a clearer foundation for future social and stay-package enhancements.

---

# Design Exploration Brief

Design Review should explore a single primary direction: a Resorts World Live orientation experience that fits into the Entertainment page hierarchy after The Theatre context and before lower-priority support content.

The exploration should evaluate how the page can:

* Clarify that The Stage at Zouk is the venue context for Resorts World Live.
* Preserve Resorts World Live visibility without treating it as a separate venue.
* Connect orientation to relevant event discovery and ticket actions.
* Improve anchor/navigation clarity for the revised page structure.
* Keep FAQ accessible while reducing interruption to venue discovery.
* Include venue-level social context only where it supports confidence and does not compete with conversion.
* Respect AEG logo/content governance as a constraint.

Design Review should not create implementation plans, telemetry plans, experiments, or Figma prompts from this artifact alone.
