## CON-01

### Concept Name

Resorts World Live Orientation Journey

### Recommendation Alignment

* REC-01
* REC-02

### Experience Concept

A discovery-oriented experience that helps guests understand Resorts World Live through its relationship to The Stage at Zouk, then connects that understanding to relevant events and ticket actions.

### User Problem Solved

Guests may see Resorts World Live in navigation and The Stage at Zouk in event cards without understanding how the two relate or where that programming fits within the broader Entertainment page.

### Component Pattern

Orientation Section

### Content Strategy

Venue and programming explanation, The Stage at Zouk naming context, event lineup references, ticket-action context, and concise relationship language that avoids treating Resorts World Live as a separate venue.

### Placement Strategy

The experience belongs in the Entertainment page journey where guests move from Theatre orientation into broader venue and event discovery. It should help bridge the gap between page-level venue hierarchy and event-level ticket decisions.

### Why It Is Different

This concept centers on explaining the Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk relationship before asking guests to choose events. Other concepts focus on browsing, planning, social proof, or stay consideration.

### Strategic Goal Supported

Clarify Entertainment page hierarchy, improve Resorts World Live positioning, strengthen The Stage at Zouk representation, and support ticket conversion through better understanding.

### Experience Description

Guests encounter a dedicated orientation moment that frames The Stage at Zouk as the venue context for Resorts World Live programming. The experience gives enough information to understand what the programming represents, then connects guests to the events and ticket actions associated with that context. The experience is not a standalone venue treatment for Resorts World Live; it is a relationship-building step within Entertainment discovery.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests understand what The Stage at Zouk is, why Resorts World Live appears in the journey, and how to continue into relevant entertainment events.

### Expected Business Outcome

Resorts World Live programming receives clearer visibility, The Stage at Zouk gains stronger context, and ticket paths benefit from reduced ambiguity.

### Benefits

* Directly addresses the central hierarchy gap.
* Preserves the constraint that Resorts World Live is associated with The Stage at Zouk.
* Creates a clearer bridge between venue discovery and event conversion.
* Supports navigation and anchor clarity.

### Risks

* Relationship language must be precise to avoid positioning Resorts World Live as a separate venue.
* The experience may require approved venue copy, imagery, and naming governance.
* If too much context is added, it could slow guests who are ready to buy tickets.

### Constraints

* Resorts World Live must not be treated as a separate venue.
* The Stage at Zouk must remain the venue context.
* Existing ticket paths must remain discoverable.
* AEG and partner-brand governance must be respected.

## CON-02

### Concept Name

Entertainment Map Of Experiences

### Recommendation Alignment

* REC-01
* REC-04

### Experience Concept

A venue-discovery experience that helps guests understand the Entertainment page as a set of connected venue and programming experiences, including Resorts World Theatre, The Stage at Zouk, Ayu Dayclub, and Zouk Nightclub.

### User Problem Solved

Guests may struggle to understand the full entertainment ecosystem because venue context is split across navigation, event cards, Theatre content, FAQ placement, and lower-page venue sections.

### Component Pattern

Experience Directory

### Content Strategy

Concise venue/programming summaries, relationship labels, atmosphere cues, event-type cues, social-context cues where available, and paths into relevant event discovery.

### Placement Strategy

The experience belongs where the page shifts from broad Entertainment introduction into venue and event exploration. It should give guests a mental model before they browse individual events or venue details.

### Why It Is Different

This concept organizes the full entertainment ecosystem rather than focusing mainly on Resorts World Live. It treats hierarchy as a map of choices across venues and experiences.

### Strategic Goal Supported

Improve Entertainment hierarchy, venue discovery continuity, navigation clarity, and social-context readiness across entertainment venues.

### Experience Description

Guests are introduced to the entertainment ecosystem as a connected set of experiences. Each venue or programming context is positioned by what guests can expect there and how it relates to event discovery. The Stage at Zouk is presented as the Resorts World Live context, while other venues retain their own identities. Social context can support atmosphere and recency where official venue channels are available.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests can quickly understand which entertainment context fits their intent before browsing deeper into events or venue pages.

### Expected Business Outcome

Broader venue consideration improves, lower-page venue content becomes easier to discover, and the page can support multiple entertainment business lines without relying only on event cards.

### Benefits

* Helps guests compare entertainment contexts at a high level.
* Supports venue discovery beyond a single section.
* Provides a natural place for venue-level social context.
* Can make anchor/navigation structure more meaningful.

### Risks

* Could create too much choice if venue distinctions are unclear.
* Requires accurate taxonomy and content ownership across venues.
* Social-context quality may vary by venue.

### Constraints

* The concept must not imply unsupported venues or unavailable programming.
* The Stage at Zouk and Resorts World Live relationship must remain clear.
* Social context must be brand-safe and maintainable.
* Ticket and booking paths must not be obscured.

## CON-03

### Concept Name

Choose Your Entertainment Path

### Recommendation Alignment

* REC-01
* REC-02
* REC-03

### Experience Concept

A guided decision-support experience that helps guests enter Entertainment discovery by intent: seeing a show, going out, finding a date, exploring a venue, or planning a stay around entertainment.

### User Problem Solved

Guests may arrive with different goals, but the current experience asks them to interpret a long page, event filters, venue labels, and mixed CTA types on their own.

### Component Pattern

Guided Selector

### Content Strategy

Intent-based labels, venue and event-type cues, action explanations, date relevance, and stay-context language that remains accurate without inventing package availability.

### Placement Strategy

The experience belongs early in the Entertainment journey, before or alongside event discovery, where it can orient guests toward the most relevant browsing path without replacing the event list.

### Why It Is Different

This concept is intent-led rather than venue-led. It starts with what the guest wants to accomplish, then connects to venues, events, ticket actions, or stay context.

### Strategic Goal Supported

Improve event discovery, venue discovery, ticket-action clarity, and entertainment-to-stay consideration.

### Experience Description

Guests are prompted to identify the type of entertainment journey they are pursuing. The experience then frames the relevant discovery path conceptually, such as event browsing, venue exploration, The Stage at Zouk/Resorts World Live discovery, or stay-oriented entertainment planning. It clarifies the kind of action guests should expect next, such as ticketing, tables, learning more, or booking a stay.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests can navigate toward the entertainment content that best matches their intent with less uncertainty about venues, event types, and commercial actions.

### Expected Business Outcome

The page can better support varied guest intents while improving continuity into ticket, table, venue, and stay-related paths.

### Benefits

* Reduces reliance on guests interpreting venue taxonomy unaided.
* Creates a flexible bridge between event discovery and stay discovery.
* Can support desktop and mobile experiences conceptually.
* Helps distinguish different commercial actions.

### Risks

* Intent categories must be validated against real guest behavior.
* Too much guidance could add friction for guests who already know what they want.
* Stay-related paths must not imply universal package availability.

### Constraints

* Existing event card discovery must remain available.
* Ticket conversion must not be diluted.
* Package and stay messaging must respect eligibility and operational constraints.
* The experience must not invent business rules or fulfillment processes.

## CON-04

### Concept Name

Event Action Clarity Experience

### Recommendation Alignment

* REC-02

### Experience Concept

A decision-support experience focused on making event actions easier to understand across tickets, tables, learn-more paths, venue booking, stay booking, and complimentary ticket actions.

### User Problem Solved

Guests may not know the practical difference between multiple CTA labels and destination types when browsing entertainment events.

### Component Pattern

Action Clarity System

### Content Strategy

Plain-language action definitions, event-type context, venue context, expected next-step language, and consistent commercial-action hierarchy.

### Placement Strategy

The experience belongs within event discovery and adjacent commercial decision points, where guests evaluate an event and decide how to proceed.

### Why It Is Different

This concept does not primarily change venue hierarchy or add new merchandising. It focuses tightly on reducing friction at the moment of action selection.

### Strategic Goal Supported

Strengthen ticket conversion support, clarify commercial action paths, and improve guest confidence during event discovery.

### Experience Description

Guests browsing events encounter clearer signals about what each action represents. The experience helps distinguish ticket purchase, table reservation, event detail, venue booking, stay booking, and complimentary access actions without prescribing a specific visual layout or implementation pattern. It keeps the event card as the core browsing surface while improving action comprehension.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests feel more confident choosing the right next step and understand when they are moving into ticketing, tables, booking, or more information.

### Expected Business Outcome

Clearer action paths can support ticket conversion, reduce misclicks or confusion, and improve future evaluation of outbound handoffs.

### Benefits

* Focuses on the highest-friction commercial decision point.
* Preserves the current event-listing foundation.
* Can apply across Resorts World Live, Theatre, nightlife, dayclub, and other event types.
* Supports future measurement discussions.

### Risks

* Third-party ticketing or table partners may constrain action labels.
* Without downstream analytics, conversion impact may be difficult to prove.
* Too much explanatory content could clutter event browsing.

### Constraints

* Ticket paths must remain prominent.
* Third-party handoffs must remain intact.
* The concept must not define analytics plans or implementation requirements.
* Action clarity must adapt to different event types.

## CON-05

### Concept Name

Concert Weekend Planning Path

### Recommendation Alignment

* REC-01
* REC-03

### Experience Concept

A planning-oriented experience that connects entertainment discovery to the possibility of building a concert weekend around eligible events, without assuming package availability for every event.

### User Problem Solved

Guests interested in entertainment may not realize when an event could be connected to a broader resort stay, especially when discovering events from the Entertainment page rather than the Resorts World Live page.

### Component Pattern

Planning Pathway

### Content Strategy

Event-date context, stay-consideration prompts, eligibility-aware language, Resorts World Live relationship context, and booking-path context that avoids implying universal packages.

### Placement Strategy

The experience belongs in the Entertainment-to-stay journey, especially near Resorts World Live or date-based event discovery moments where travel planning intent may be relevant.

### Why It Is Different

This concept treats entertainment as part of trip planning. It is not primarily about venue hierarchy or CTA clarity; it explores the stay relationship created by event interest.

### Strategic Goal Supported

Support stay-package visibility, entertainment-to-room consideration, and Resorts World Live cross-promotion while preserving ticket conversion.

### Experience Description

Guests discovering relevant entertainment are given a conceptual path to consider the event as part of a weekend at Resorts World. The experience frames stay relevance around event dates and Resorts World Live programming, while respecting that package eligibility and booking rules may vary. It does not invent a package builder or assume all events have stay offers.

### Expected Guest Outcome

Guests with travel intent can understand that entertainment may connect to a broader stay opportunity and know how to continue exploring that possibility.

### Expected Business Outcome

Entertainment interest can support room-night consideration, Resorts World Live merchandising, and future stay-package strategy.

### Benefits

* Builds on the existing `Concert Weekend Stay` pattern from the Resorts World Live page.
* Connects entertainment demand to resort booking potential.
* Supports date-aware discovery without requiring a full package engine concept.
* Keeps stay-promotion exploration grounded in operational constraints.

### Risks

* Could distract from ticket purchase if not carefully balanced.
* Package eligibility, inventory, and routing may be operationally complex.
* Guests may expect offers that are not available if language is too broad.

### Constraints

* Do not assume package inventory.
* Do not invent booking-engine functionality.
* Ticket conversion remains protected.
* Stay messaging must be accurate, date-aware, and operationally maintainable.
