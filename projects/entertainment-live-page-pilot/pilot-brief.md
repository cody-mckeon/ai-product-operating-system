# Pilot Overview

This pilot will evaluate the Entertainment page experience for Resorts World Las Vegas, with an initial focus on how Resorts World Live and venue experiences should be represented on the page.

Primary pilot question:

How should the Entertainment page merchandise Resorts World Live and venue experiences while improving discovery and supporting ticket conversion?

# Business Context

The Entertainment page is a key discovery point for guests exploring shows, venues, and ticketed experiences at Resorts World Las Vegas.

Stakeholders have identified a need to clarify the relationship between Entertainment page content, AEG branding, The Theatre, and Resorts World Live. Resorts World Live is associated with The Stage at Zouk and should not be treated as a separate venue.

The page also needs to support venue discovery, ticket conversion, and future consideration of social media integrations across entertainment venues.

# Stakeholder Requests

1. Remove AEG content from Entertainment page.
2. Add AEG logo placement on Entertainment page.
3. Add a new Resorts World Live section after The Theatre section.
4. The Stage at Zouk should be the H2 heading for the Resorts World Live section.
5. Move FAQ to the bottom of the page.
6. Explore social media integrations for all entertainment venues.

# Problem Statement

The current Entertainment page needs clearer content hierarchy and venue merchandising to support discovery and ticket conversion. Stakeholder feedback indicates that AEG content, AEG logo placement, Resorts World Live, The Stage at Zouk, FAQ placement, and social media integrations all need to be evaluated as part of the initial pilot input.

# Goals

* Clarify how Resorts World Live should appear within the Entertainment page experience.
* Ensure The Stage at Zouk is treated as the H2 heading for the Resorts World Live section.
* Preserve the known relationship between Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk.
* Identify page-structure requirements that support entertainment discovery.
* Capture stakeholder requirements for AEG content, AEG logo placement, FAQ placement, and social media integrations.
* Establish initial pilot inputs for later analysis and recommendation work.

# Success Criteria

* The pilot brief accurately captures the stakeholder requests.
* The pilot scope reflects the known relationship between Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk.
* The Entertainment page and Resorts World Live page are documented as source URLs.
* Open questions are clearly identified for future discovery.
* No recommendations, concepts, or design directions are generated in this input stage.

# In Scope

* Entertainment page content hierarchy.
* Resorts World Live representation on the Entertainment page.
* The Stage at Zouk heading treatment within the Resorts World Live section.
* AEG content removal requirements.
* AEG logo placement requirements.
* FAQ placement on the Entertainment page.
* Exploration requirements for social media integrations across entertainment venues.
* Source URL collection for current page review.

# Out Of Scope

* Final recommendations.
* Generated concepts.
* Visual design exploration.
* Copywriting finalization.
* CMS implementation.
* Engineering implementation.
* Analytics implementation.
* Ticketing platform changes.
* Treating Resorts World Live as a separate venue from The Stage at Zouk.

# Open Questions

* What AEG content currently exists on the Entertainment page and should be removed?
* Where should the AEG logo be placed, and what brand or legal requirements govern its placement?
* What content should be included in the new Resorts World Live section?
* Which assets, event feeds, or venue descriptions are approved for The Stage at Zouk?
* What is the current FAQ placement, and what content should remain when it moves to the bottom of the page?
* Which entertainment venues should be included in social media integration exploration?
* Which social platforms are priority channels for each entertainment venue?
* Are additional venue URLs needed before current-state analysis begins?
* What ticket conversion actions should be considered primary for Entertainment page measurement?

# Initial Hypotheses

* Clarifying the relationship between Resorts World Live and The Stage at Zouk may reduce guest confusion.
* Moving FAQ content to the bottom of the page may improve the page hierarchy for venue and event discovery.
* A dedicated Resorts World Live section after The Theatre section may improve visibility for The Stage at Zouk.
* AEG logo placement may satisfy partner visibility needs without relying on broader AEG content on the page.
* Social media integrations may help guests evaluate venue energy, upcoming events, and entertainment relevance before purchasing tickets.
