# Verbatim Stakeholder Request

> We need to begin thinking about consolidating all Social Groups / Private Dining business into a single, cohesive landing page.

# Parsed Requirements

- Consider consolidation of Social Groups / Private Dining business into one cohesive landing-page experience.
- Treat Group Dining and Zouk Private Events as one unified department in the intended business story.
- Account for the full stated portfolio rather than presenting individual offerings as isolated experiences.
- Represent a unified team capable of planning different types of social experiences across the property.
- Account for one clear inquiry path as part of the business question.
- Include dedicated space for the new Social Groups Hospitality Packages scheduled to launch on August 4.
- Include the stakeholder's Zouk Private Events redesign reference in later evidence gathering.

# Parsed Business Drivers

- Improve the coherence of how the Social Groups / Private Dining portfolio is represented.
- Support demand associated with engagements, weddings, holiday parties, and other social events.
- Capture in-suite hospitality business associated with F1 and NFR.
- Make the new Social Groups Hospitality Packages visible within the scoped experience.
- Communicate cross-property planning capability through a unified-team story.
- Reduce emphasis on isolated offerings in favor of the relationship among the portfolio areas.

# Parsed Timing Pressures

- The stakeholder wants the project on the radar sooner rather than later.
- Fall is identified as peak season for engagements, weddings, holiday parties, and other social events.
- F1 and NFR create event-linked windows for in-suite hospitality demand.
- New Social Groups Hospitality Packages are scheduled to launch on August 4.
- The exact year, publication deadline, required lead time, F1 dates, and NFR dates have not yet been specified and require validation.

# Parsed Content Needs

- Group Dining.
- Zouk Private Events.
- Social Groups offerings.
- Small room blocks.
- In-suite food and beverage hospitality packages.
- Weddings.
- Social events and celebrations.
- Milestone occasions.

- F1 in-suite hospitality opportunities.
- NFR in-suite hospitality opportunities.
- Dedicated coverage for the new Social Groups Hospitality Packages launching August 4.
- Evidence supporting the story of one unified team across the property.
- Evidence concerning the current and desired inquiry journey.
- The recently redesigned Zouk Private Events experience as an aesthetic reference to investigate.

# Parsed Strategic Intent

- Reframe a broad collection of social-event and hospitality offerings as a connected business portfolio.
- Present Group Dining and Zouk Private Events as a unified department.
- Emphasize coordinated planning across the property rather than spotlighting offerings only in isolation.
- Establish that customers can engage one team for varied social experiences.
- Clarify how prospective customers begin an inquiry across the portfolio.
- Prepare the business to support near-term package visibility and seasonal demand.

This document records stakeholder input for the pilot. It does not define a recommendation, concept, information architecture, or design.
