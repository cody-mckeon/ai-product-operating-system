# Concept Selection

## Executive Summary

**Select CON-01 — One Team, Many Occasions as the primary concept for Design Review.** It is the clearest expression of the stakeholder's central request and REC-01: a single cross-property planning relationship that helps guests understand a broad social portfolio without requiring them to navigate internal departments, choose a venue first, or assume that every service shares one operating path.

The selected direction should not advance as CON-01 alone. It should retain four bounded supporting layers:

- **CON-02 — Plan by Occasion** contributes the guest-centered discovery logic.
- **CON-05 — Proven by the Planning Team** contributes team, event, property, and operational proof.
- **CON-06 — Guided Planning Concierge** contributes the separation of inspiration, qualification, and inquiry while keeping routing conditional.
- **CON-07 — Packages in Context** contributes a governed, prominent but non-dominant home for confirmed Social Groups Hospitality Packages and event-window relevance.

CON-03 — Social Experience Portfolio and CON-04 — Cross-Property Social Journey should be parked for the current exploration. Service-led organization risks exposing internal complexity and depends on an incomplete capability inventory. Journey-led organization risks implying bundled or coordinated itineraries that the current operating evidence cannot substantiate.

This decision prioritizes guest understanding and a credible one-team proposition over package merchandising, service comparison, or conversion mechanics. It also preserves the strongest observed evidence split: RWLV supplies the property frame, breadth, editorial restraint, and operational clarity; Zouk Private Sales supplies the most useful team, proof, portfolio, and private-sales storytelling behaviors.

No separate `outputs/concept-evaluation.md` was supplied. The evaluation below therefore applies the criteria explicitly requested for this selection task to the documented concepts and evidence set.

## Selection Criteria

The concepts were assessed qualitatively against the following criteria:

1. **Stakeholder and recommendation fit:** Direct support for the requested unified landing page and REC-01 through REC-04.
2. **Guest understanding:** Ability to answer who can help, what can be planned, and how the guest begins without knowledge of internal structure.
3. **One-team credibility:** Strength of the unified planning proposition without implying one person or department fulfills every service.
4. **Portfolio coverage and occasion clarity:** Ability to include the full requested portfolio while retaining a guest-centered, legible hierarchy.
5. **Inquiry truth:** Clear separation of inspiration, qualification, and inquiry without assuming a universal form, owner, or back-end route.
6. **Package and event-window resilience:** Ability to give packages, fall, F1, NFR, weddings, engagements, holidays, and milestones timely visibility without making the page temporary.
7. **Brand and storytelling fit:** Preservation of RWLV property credibility with behavioral adaptation of Zouk's team and proof strengths.
8. **Operational feasibility:** Dependence on unresolved team ownership, routing, package definitions, service relationships, or technical capabilities.
9. **Content governance and maintainability:** Need for approved proof, taxonomy, package facts, content ownership, expiration rules, and duplication control.
10. **Overpromise and structural risk:** Likelihood of becoming a venue directory, campaign page, internal department page, meetings reskin, or unsupported bundled experience.

The most important business problems, in order, are:

1. Establish a credible unified planning relationship.
2. Make the broad portfolio understandable through guest need and occasion.
3. Create a truthful transition from inspiration to qualification and inquiry.
4. Prove RWLV breadth and specialist capability.
5. Accommodate packages and event windows within an evergreen system.

## Tier 1 Concepts

### CON-01 — One Team, Many Occasions

CON-01 is the only concept recommended as a complete primary direction for Design Review.

It best answers the business question because it makes the planning relationship the stable organizing idea while allowing guest occasions, services, packages, venues, proof, and event windows to play distinct supporting roles. It covers the full portfolio without flattening it into an equal-weight list and remains viable even while routing and package details are unresolved, provided those unknowns are represented honestly.

Its primary design-review challenge is credibility: the one-team proposition must read as continuity of planning supported by specialists, not as a claim that one individual, department, form, or operating system fulfills every need.

## Tier 2 Concepts

### CON-02 — Plan by Occasion

Retain as the principal discovery layer within CON-01. Occasion and guest need should help people recognize fit before they encounter service or venue detail. This layer should not become a set of duplicated, self-contained occasion pages within the landing experience, and it must not imply that every related capability is automatically available as a bundle.

### CON-05 — Proven by the Planning Team

Retain as the trust and evidence layer. It contributes the strongest useful behavior from Zouk Private Sales: visible expertise, past-event evidence, client or event proof where approved, portfolio confidence, and operational specificity. It should validate the unified proposition rather than replace orientation with a gallery of proof. RWLV property context must remain dominant, and evidence must cover more than nightlife where assets permit.

### CON-06 — Guided Planning Concierge

Retain as the inquiry framework, not as a prescribed interactive product or universal form. It contributes a clear sequence from inspiration to qualification to inquiry and provides a way to ask what the guest is planning before selecting an operational destination. Design Review should keep multiple routing outcomes possible until the owner, fields, handoffs, and fallback paths are confirmed.

### CON-07 — Packages in Context

Retain conditionally as the package and event-window layer. It provides dedicated visibility for confirmed Social Groups Hospitality Packages while preserving the broader planning proposition and a custom-planning path. It should advance only at the fidelity supported by approved facts. Until the year and meaning of August 4, package details, ownership, terms, availability, imagery, and route are confirmed, it remains a governed placeholder responsibility rather than a fully expressible offer experience.

## Rejected Or Parked Concepts

### CON-03 — Social Experience Portfolio

Park as a primary direction. Its service-led model is useful for internal content governance and may later inform secondary capability explanations, but it asks guests to understand categories before their occasion. It also depends on missing definitions for room blocks, in-suite hospitality, wellness, bachelorette, Athena photography, and cross-team ownership. As a dominant model, it has the highest risk of becoming an internal department or service inventory page.

### CON-04 — Cross-Property Social Journey

Park for a future phase after the business can substantiate coordinated multi-moment planning. It provides an evocative way to show RWLV breadth, but its implied sequence can be mistaken for a defined itinerary, commercially available bundle, or operating promise. It may also add unnecessary complexity for guests seeking one dinner, one event, or one package. Selected journey evidence could later support storytelling, but the journey should not organize the initial design exploration.

No concept is rejected as inherently unusable. CON-03 and CON-04 are excluded from current Design Review because they solve lower-priority problems and carry greater evidence or overpromise risk than the selected direction.

## Recommended Combined Direction

### Primary Concept

**CON-01 — One Team, Many Occasions**

The experience should be evaluated as the primary orientation and inquiry entry for social planning across Resorts World Las Vegas. Its central proposition is one continuous planning relationship supported by relevant specialists across the property.

### Supporting Concept Layers

| Supporting concept | Contribution to the combined direction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CON-02 — Plan by Occasion | Guest-centered recognition and discovery | Occasion labels remain provisional; capabilities cannot be presented as automatic bundles. |
| CON-05 — Proven by the Planning Team | Team credibility, event proof, property evidence, and operational detail | Only approved, representative evidence may be used; Zouk behavior does not become the overall visual identity. |
| CON-06 — Guided Planning Concierge | Separation of inspiration, qualification, and inquiry | No universal form, automated routing, owner, SLA, or technical behavior is assumed. |
| CON-07 — Packages in Context | Dedicated package visibility and maintainable event-window relevance | No package facts, timing, availability, terms, or destination are expressed beyond approved inputs. |

### What Each Layer Contributes

- **CON-01 supplies the stable story:** who helps and why the portfolio belongs together.
- **CON-02 supplies the primary orientation logic:** what the guest is planning or needs.
- **CON-05 supplies confidence:** why the team and property should be trusted.
- **CON-06 supplies progression:** how understanding becomes a qualified planning conversation.
- **CON-07 supplies current commercial relevance:** where confirmed packages and time-sensitive demand can appear without redefining the page.

### What Should Be Avoided

- Converting the combined direction into a collage of five competing concepts.
- Giving team, occasion, service, venue, package, and season equal hierarchy.
- Presenting internal departments as the guest's primary navigation model.
- Using a flat venue or offer grid as the main expression of breadth.
- Treating Zouk styling, nightlife imagery, or venue density as the page-wide visual system.
- Treating the current RWLV Meetings & Events page as a template to reskin.
- Making August 4, fall, F1, NFR, or holiday content the permanent page identity.
- Implying that related services are packaged, available, or fulfilled together without confirmation.
- Sending every guest to the current default Meetings & Events RFP or any other single destination without routing validation.

## Design Review Inputs

Design Review should evaluate the combined direction against the following specific questions:

1. Does the experience make the one-team planning relationship understandable before presenting portfolio detail?
2. Is that promise framed as continuity supported by specialists rather than as unsupported organizational or operational unity?
3. Can guests recognize their occasion or planning need without knowing a venue, service category, or department?
4. Does the direction cover Group Dining, Zouk Private Events, Social Groups, small room blocks, in-suite hospitality, Weddings, celebrations, milestones, bachelorette, wellness, Athena engagement photography, F1, NFR, and Social Groups Hospitality Packages without giving every item equal weight?
5. Are services and venues used as explanatory context and proof rather than as the dominant taxonomy?
6. Does RWLV remain the unmistakable property frame through warmth, editorial restraint, environmental imagery, hierarchy, and operational clarity?
7. Are Zouk's strongest behaviors—human expertise, immersive event evidence, portfolio confidence, and concrete detail—adapted without cloning its styling or applying nightlife intensity to all occasions?
8. Is proof placed close to the team or capability claim it supports, and can the direction degrade honestly when approved proof is unavailable?
9. Are inspiration, qualification, and inquiry visibly distinct responsibilities without prescribing a final component, form, or route?
10. Can the inquiry posture support shared intake, category branching, manual triage, or handoff to an existing destination until stakeholders confirm the model?
11. Can packages receive dedicated prominence without becoming the page's organizing principle, and is there a credible state when no confirmed package is publishable?
12. Can fall, F1, NFR, holidays, wedding season, and engagement demand be emphasized, updated, and retired without disrupting the evergreen page identity?
13. Does the content hierarchy avoid a dense equal-weight card wall and preserve one primary focal point at a time?
14. Does the direction remain understandable when the experience becomes sequential on mobile, with concise labels, legible proof, and clear action intent?
15. Which claims or sections must remain provisional because their operating, content, or governance dependencies are unresolved?

## Open Questions To Carry Forward

### Package Details

- What are the approved package names, audiences, guest value, inclusions, exclusions, eligibility rules, pricing posture, terms, inventory, room or suite requirements, capacities, imagery, and proof?
- Are packages inquiry-led, reservable, or subject to qualification, and who owns each next step?
- What content appears when a package is unavailable, expired, or not yet approved?

### August 4 Timing

- Which year does August 4 refer to?
- Is it the content publication deadline, commercial launch, availability start, sales-readiness milestone, or a combination?
- What approvals, assets, routing, and operational readiness must precede that date?

### Team Ownership

- Which organization or role owns the unified guest relationship?
- Which Group Dining, Zouk Private Sales, Social Groups, Weddings, hotel sales, F&B, Concierge, wellness, and photography specialists participate?
- What can the team truthfully coordinate, where do handoffs occur, and how is continuity communicated?
- Are named people, role-based expertise, or collective team framing approved, and who maintains them?

### Inquiry Routing

- Is the operating model a shared form, category-specific forms, manual team triage, or a clear front door with handoffs to existing destinations?
- Which qualification information is necessary for each intent, and how is source context preserved?
- Who receives, acknowledges, routes, and follows up on multi-service requests?
- What alternatives exist for hotel booking, restaurant reservation, Concierge, phone, email, and chat, and how are their purposes distinguished?

### Proof Assets

- Which team portraits, biographies, event images, client marks, testimonials, venue facts, capacities, floor plans, and cross-team case evidence are approved for this page?
- Are proof rights and approvals valid across both RWLV and Zouk contexts?
- Is there representative proof for Weddings, room blocks, in-suite hospitality, wellness, bachelorette, Athena engagement photography, F1, and NFR, or only for Zouk and venue-led events?
- Who owns freshness, legal review, accessibility treatment, and retirement of proof?

### Mobile Behavior

- How should the broad portfolio and occasion discovery become sequential without losing the relationship among team, need, service, proof, and inquiry?
- How do long team stories, event proof, package details, qualification, and alternate inquiry paths remain scannable and accessible on smaller screens?
- What mobile states exist for the current RWLV RFP, Zouk proposal experience, anchors, forms, and proof modules?

### Brand Approvals

- What is the approved RWLV/Zouk hierarchy, attribution, naming, and cross-domain relationship?
- Which Zouk team, proof, imagery, and private-sales behaviors may be adapted within an RWLV property experience?
- How should nightlife energy be bounded so Weddings, wellness, engagements, dining, and hospitality retain appropriate tone?
- Which final assets, typography, colors, and component treatments require RWLV Brand, Zouk Brand, Legal, or partner approval?
