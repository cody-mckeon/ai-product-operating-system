## Approved Intent Baseline

The approved direction remains **CON-01 — One Team, Many Occasions**, expressed
as a dedicated RWLV-framed orientation and inquiry entry. The experience should
lead with one continuous planning relationship supported by relevant
specialists, then help guests recognize their occasion or practical need.
Services, venues, team proof, packages, seasons, qualification, and inquiry are
supporting responsibilities rather than equal organizing systems. Sources:
`outputs/concept-selection.md` and `outputs/design-review.md`.

The approved hierarchy remains:

1. Unified planning relationship.
2. Guest occasion or planning need.
3. Confirmed capabilities and RWLV property breadth.
4. Approved team, event, property, and operational proof.
5. Confirmed package and event-window relevance.
6. Qualification context and truthful inquiry.

DR-01 and DR-02 are approved for exploration. DR-03, DR-04, and DR-05 remain
conditional. No artifact may imply verified team scope, publishable package
facts, approved proof, one universal inquiry route, or final CTA behavior while
those dependencies remain unresolved. Sources: `outputs/design-review.md` and
`outputs/figma-prompts.md`.

The v1 review required v2 to remove false verification signals, replace the
equal card wall, preserve the existing RWLV frame, govern imagery and proof,
keep capability and team scope provisional, show honest package and no-package
states, neutralize routing copy, supply mobile evidence, and improve contrast
and RWLV specificity. Sources: `outputs/design-artifact-review-v1.md` and
`outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md`.

## What Improved From v1

- V2 removes the invented full global header, `Reservations`, and `Plan an
  Event` controls shown in v1. It no longer directly proposes replacement
  global navigation in the captured hero.
- The Bond Street mark is gone, reducing one clear cross-brand and rights risk.
- The occasion section has more hierarchy than v1: one large feature, two
  secondary features, and three smaller items replace five more uniformly
  treated cards.
- V2 removes `Verified specialists` and repeated `[VERIFIED CAPABILITY]` labels.
  Team, role, capability, image, and proof annotations more often say `pending
  approval`, `pending review`, or `requires validation`.
- The package section improves materially. It replaces three pseudo-offer cards
  with one governed package-readiness structure, explicitly says the content is
  not an active offer, groups the facts that require confirmation, and states
  that routing must not be assumed universal.
- The planning-guide section explicitly says it is guidance rather than a
  universal intake form.
- The final section continues to distinguish planning inquiry from hotel
  reservations, table reservations, and property support.
- Most mid-page sections place body copy on stable white surfaces instead of
  over imagery, improving basic readability over v1.
- The page remains within the approved landing-page role and preserves the
  seven-stage journey instead of drifting into a venue directory, long form,
  Zouk microsite, or seasonal campaign.

## What Regressed From v1

- `occasion-need.png` contains a broken primary image. The visible alternative
  text `Elegant wedding table setting` appears at the top of the largest
  feature area. This weakens the page's primary discovery layer and reveals an
  ungoverned image description as UI.
- V2 removes some false verification labels but replaces them with substantially
  more realistic final-sounding consumer copy. Detailed claims now appear
  across occasion, capability, team, package, planning-guide, and CTA sections,
  making pending annotations easier to overlook.
- The property-breadth layer expands from six short capability statements into
  four columns of specific, unconfirmed services, including room blocks,
  in-suite arrival curation, spa programming, Athena photography, F1/NFR
  hospitality, and availability guidance.
- The planning guide expands four v1 topics into six detailed statements that
  imply known qualification logic and service availability.
- V2 introduces more final-looking CTA labels: `START PLANNING`, `EXPLORE
  OCCASIONS`, `INQUIRE ABOUT PACKAGES`, and `SUBMIT A PLANNING INQUIRY`. Small
  pending notes do not neutralize the action labels' apparent authority.
- The hero and final planning section use very pale image overlays with small
  black annotation text, retaining contrast risk and reducing visual clarity.
- No mobile evidence was added despite mobile being a named v1 acceptance
  requirement.

## What Still Needs Work

- The page still treats unresolved team, capability, package, seasonal, and
  inquiry material as realistic consumer content rather than role-based
  placeholders.
- The occasion layer remains a six-item image grid and fixed taxonomy. The
  asymmetry improves hierarchy but does not satisfy the approved curated
  editorial orientation.
- The existing RWLV global frame is absent from the v2 evidence. Removal of the
  invented v1 header is an improvement, but preservation of the actual header,
  footer, and site context remains unverified.
- V2 imagery still lacks source, rights, freshness, representative-coverage,
  and approval evidence. Pending labels are helpful but do not make the images
  approved proof.
- Team and proof claims remain too specific. `Planned with the right people at
  every step`, `Social Groups Planning`, and the three role groupings imply an
  operating model that has not been confirmed.
- The package module remains incomplete as a governed state. It says packages
  exist, uses `INQUIRE ABOUT PACKAGES`, and does not visibly present a separate
  evergreen no-approved-package/custom-planning fallback.
- Planning-guide content still reads as a confirmed field and decision model,
  including F1/NFR timing, room blocks, suite coordination, private dining,
  in-suite F&B, spa, photography, Concierge, and amenities.
- CTA copy and destinations remain unconfirmed throughout the artifact.
- No mobile sequence, interaction states, or accessibility test evidence is
  available.
- RWLV visual specificity remains limited. The page is restrained and
  editorial, but largely black-and-white with bold/italic sans-serif display
  type and no observed restrained red action system or clearly preserved site
  frame.

## What Must Change

1. Replace realistic unconfirmed consumer copy with explicit content-role
   placeholders. Pending annotations cannot sit beneath copy that already makes
   the unresolved claim.
2. Fix or remove the broken primary occasion image. Do not expose asset alt text
   as visible interface content.
3. Recompose occasion/need discovery so it is not a fixed six-item image grid.
   Use editorial hierarchy and keep taxonomy visibly provisional.
4. Remove specific service and operating claims from Planning Support unless a
   cited approved source confirms each statement. Preserve only the
   responsibility to explain confirmed breadth.
5. Recast Team & Proof as a conditional evidence plan. Do not name an apparent
   Social Groups Planning function, private-dining coordination group, or
   hospitality/experience group without approved ownership and role evidence.
6. Keep package content structurally governed: remove `INQUIRE ABOUT PACKAGES`,
   make the action fully pending, and show a distinct no-approved-package state
   with route-neutral custom planning.
7. Reduce the Planning Guide to requirement-level topics. Do not imply final
   fields, qualification logic, service availability, event-window rules, or
   routing.
8. Replace final-looking `START PLANNING`, `EXPLORE OCCASIONS`, and `SUBMIT A
   PLANNING INQUIRY` labels with explicit pending action roles unless the exact
   labels and destinations are approved.
9. Demonstrate preservation of the actual RWLV global frame or clearly label it
   as a locked existing component outside the design exploration.
10. Provide a complete mobile v3 sequence and stable desktop/mobile evidence
    for the next review.
11. Improve contrast and readability in the hero and final image-led section,
    including small annotations and action text.

## What Should Not Be Changed

- Keep CON-01 and the approved page role.
- Keep the current seven-stage journey order.
- Keep the unified planning relationship and occasion/need recognition as the
  two primary responsibilities.
- Keep capability, proof, packages, qualification, and inquiry subordinate.
- Keep packages after orientation and trust.
- Keep the improved single governed package-readiness structure rather than
  returning to three pseudo-offer cards.
- Keep the distinction among planning inquiry, hotel reservations, table
  reservations, and property support.
- Keep the explicit statements that package content is not active and that no
  universal intake or route should be assumed.
- Keep the Bond Street mark removed.
- Keep the non-Zouk, non-nightlife, non-campaign posture.
- Keep generous spacing and chapter rhythm; correct truthfulness and hierarchy
  without replacing the concept.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1 — Unresolved Scope Still Reads As Consumer Truth

- **Dimension:** Content Truthfulness; Strategy Alignment; Content Governance
- **Observation:** V2 adds pending notes but presents extensive final-sounding
  claims about one relationship, specialist help, property-wide planning,
  capability breadth, team roles, packages, and qualification.
- **Expected state:** The visible composition must use unmistakable content-role
  placeholders for unresolved claims. An annotation cannot legitimize copy that
  already asserts the unknown fact.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** All v2 captures, especially `hero.png`,
  `planning-support.png`, `team-proof.png`, and `planning-guide.png`.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Content Status
  Inputs and Content Governance; `outputs/figma-prompts.md` — constraints;
  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision 1.
- **Impact:** Stakeholders or vendors may treat provisional scope, services, and
  behavior as approved content.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Replace claim-level copy with role-based pending
  states until a named source and owner approve it.

### Finding 2 — Occasion Discovery Remains A Fixed Grid And Adds A Broken Asset

- **Dimension:** Approved Hierarchy; Component And Pattern Fit; Accessibility
  Risks
- **Observation:** The section is still a six-item image grid, and its largest
  image is broken with visible alternative text.
- **Expected state:** Curated editorial occasion/need orientation with
  provisional taxonomy, reliable asset behavior, and no equal destination wall.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** `occasion-need.png` shows a large Weddings &
  Engagements feature, two medium features, three small features, and visible
  `Elegant wedding table setting` broken-image text.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Occasion / Need
  Discovery Review; `outputs/figma-prompts.md` — Prompts 1 and 2;
  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision 2.
- **Impact:** The taxonomy appears fixed, related services look bundled, and the
  most important discovery feature is visually broken.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Replace the grid with a more editorial hierarchy,
  keep labels pending, and supply a governed working asset or neutral image-role
  placeholder.

### Finding 3 — Capability Detail Still Overstates Availability And Coordination

- **Dimension:** Strategy Alignment; Content Truthfulness; Package And Seasonal
  Treatment
- **Observation:** Planning Support lists detailed services and coordination
  behavior despite a small note saying descriptions require validation.
- **Expected state:** Property breadth is a content responsibility until each
  capability, owner, availability condition, and relationship is approved.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** `planning-support.png` names private dining
  reservations, buyouts, menu planning, room blocks, in-suite arrival curation,
  Concierge logistics, spa, Athena photography, itinerary support, F1/NFR
  hospitality, and availability guidance.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Supporting Portfolio
  Layer and Content Governance; `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision
  5.
- **Impact:** The page can imply confirmed availability, fulfillment, and
  coordinated service bundles that the evidence does not establish.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Replace detailed claims with capability-role
  placeholders or individually cite approved source evidence.

### Finding 4 — Team And Proof Still Implies An Unconfirmed Operating Model

- **Dimension:** Proof Integrity; Content Truthfulness; Brand Fit
- **Observation:** Pending notes are present, but the section names apparent
  planning functions and says specialists support every step.
- **Expected state:** Collective or role-based placeholders that do not establish
  a department, ownership model, or universal coverage; proof must be sourced
  and adjacent to the exact claim it supports.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** `team-proof.png` uses `Planned with the right
  people at every step`, `Social Groups Planning`, `Private Dining
  Coordination`, `Hospitality & Experiences`, and ungoverned people/event
  imagery.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Team Framing Review
  and Proof And Trust Review; `outputs/figma-prompts.md` — Prompt 3;
  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revisions 4 and 6.
- **Impact:** The artifact suggests that a cross-property team structure and
  scope have been verified.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Return titles, scope, and proof to neutral pending
  roles; retain the evidence architecture without asserting the organization.

### Finding 5 — Package Governance Improved But CTA And Fallback Remain Wrong

- **Dimension:** Package And Seasonal Treatment; Inquiry Honesty; CTA Clarity
- **Observation:** The single readiness module is a substantial improvement, but
  it uses an actual package inquiry label and does not show a distinct evergreen
  no-approved-package/custom-planning state.
- **Expected state:** Package and action content remain fully pending; active and
  no-approved-package responsibilities are distinct, and custom planning does
  not assume the same route.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** `packages-relevance.png` says `PACKAGE CONTENT
  PENDING — NOT ACTIVE OFFERS` but also uses `INQUIRE ABOUT PACKAGES` and a
  package-existence statement.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Package And
  Seasonal Content Review; `outputs/figma-prompts.md` — Prompt 4;
  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision 7.
- **Impact:** Reviewers may infer a package route or publishable offer category
  before facts and ownership exist.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Preserve the single governed structure, replace the
  action with a pending role, and add the explicit fallback/custom-planning
  state.

### Finding 6 — Planning Guide Encodes Unverified Qualification Logic

- **Dimension:** Inquiry Honesty; Guest Clarity; Content Governance Risks
- **Observation:** Six detailed topics are presented as the information that
  guides planning, including examples and consequences.
- **Expected state:** A minimal set of requirement-level placeholders that does
  not become approved fields, routing logic, service availability, or a hidden
  universal intake model.
- **Evidence from the artifact:** `planning-guide.png` names occasion type,
  preferred timing, group size, accommodation needs, dining/hospitality
  priorities, and optional enhancements with detailed service examples.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — Inquiry And CTA
  Review; `outputs/figma-prompts.md` — Prompt 5;
  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision 8.
- **Impact:** The design can prematurely settle qualification requirements and
  imply routes or services that owners have not validated.
- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Keep the guidance structure but return topic names,
  examples, and consequences to pending requirement labels.

- **Dimension:** CTA Clarity; Inquiry Honesty
- **Observation:** V2 repeatedly shows specific actions while nearby notes say

- **Expected state:** Action roles remain explicit placeholders until labels,

- **Evidence from the artifact:** `hero.png` uses `START PLANNING` and `EXPLORE
  OCCASIONS`; `packages-relevance.png` uses `INQUIRE ABOUT PACKAGES`;
  `begin-planning.png` uses `SUBMIT A PLANNING INQUIRY`.
- **Source of approved intent:** `outputs/design-review.md` — CTA Hierarchy;

  `outputs/design-revision-notes-v1.md` — Revision 8.
- **Impact:** The page looks operationally complete and may drive premature

- **Severity:** Major
- **Required disposition:** Use pending action-role labels only, while
  preserving the separation among planning, booking, reservation, and support.

## Readiness Decision

**Status: Major Revision.**

V2 is closer to the approved structural direction than v1. It improves package
governance, removes the Bond Street mark and invented global controls, uses
clearer pending annotations, and retains the correct page role and journey.
Those improvements should be protected.

However, the artifact still contains major content-truth, hierarchy, proof,
package, inquiry, responsive, and accessibility issues. Most unresolved
material has been rendered as realistic consumer copy, and the required mobile
evidence remains absent. The broken primary occasion image is also a visible
regression. V2 is suitable only for another internal revision cycle. It is not
ready for stakeholder approval or Vendor Handoff.

Proceed to a bounded v3 revision. If v3 cannot remain honest without deciding
team ownership, taxonomy, package scope, or inquiry routing, stop the artifact
loop and return those decisions to Design Review or the responsible
stakeholders.
