## Blocking And Major Findings

- Unconfirmed team and capability scope is presented as verified.
- Occasion/need discovery uses the explicitly prohibited equal-card-wall model.
- Global navigation and actions are not demonstrably preserved from the
  existing RWLV experience.
- Actual-looking imagery and marks function as approved team, property, event,
  or occasion evidence without governance.
- Package cards imply an `INQUIRE` route and omit the no-approved-package
  fallback.
- Planning-context statements imply unverified routing and operational facts.
- Required mobile exploration is absent.
- Text-over-image contrast and legibility present material accessibility risk.

## Minor Findings

- The restrained composition does not yet express enough observed RWLV visual
  specificity.
- A full-page capture or Figma reference would make sequence and component
  continuity easier to verify.
- Internal annotations need a more consistent separation from hypothetical
  consumer-facing content.

## Verification Gaps

- Mobile and tablet states
- Full-page sequence and transition behavior
- Figma node, component structure, and editability
- Navigation, anchor, CTA, and package-action destinations
- Hover, focus, keyboard, error, validation, success, unavailable, expired, and
  handoff states
- Image, mark, team, biography, event, and proof sources and rights
- Approved team scope and capability ownership
- Package facts, August 4 year/meaning, seasonal rules, and inactive state
- Inquiry model, fields, privacy, routing, owner, fallback, and response
  expectation
- Measured contrast, semantic structure, alternative text, and reduced-motion
  behavior

## Gate Decision

**Major Revision.** Revise within the approved CON-01 / DR-01–DR-05 direction
and review the new artifact again. Do not proceed to stakeholder approval or
Vendor Handoff. Return to Design Review only if revision requires a new team,
taxonomy, package, routing, or page-role decision rather than correction of the
documented drift.
