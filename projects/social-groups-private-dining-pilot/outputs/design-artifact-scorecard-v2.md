## Blocking And Major Findings

- Realistic unconfirmed consumer copy continues to assert team, capability,
  service, package, seasonal, qualification, and inquiry scope.
- Occasion discovery remains a fixed image grid and its primary image is broken.
- Planning Support lists unconfirmed services and coordination behavior.
- Team & Proof implies an unconfirmed organization and uses ungoverned evidence.
- Package CTA and no-package fallback remain unresolved despite improved module
  structure.
- Planning Guide encodes unconfirmed qualification logic and service examples.
- Final-looking CTA labels contradict pending destinations and routing.
- Required mobile evidence remains absent.

## Minor Findings

- The actual RWLV global frame remains unverified in the v2 capture set.
- Brand expression remains generic relative to the observed RWLV visual system.
- Hero and final image-led sections need stronger contrast and clearer status
  hierarchy.
- No full-page capture or Figma node verifies continuity and editability.

## Verification Gaps

- Complete mobile and tablet states
- Full-page desktop sequence
- Figma node, component structure, annotations, and editability
- Actual RWLV header, footer, subnavigation, and site integration
- Image, mark, team, event, property, and proof sources and rights
- Approved taxonomy, team model, capability boundaries, and service availability
- Package facts, August 4 meaning/year, seasonal rules, fallback, owner, and route
- CTA labels, destinations, fields, privacy, routing, response, and handoff
- Focus, keyboard, semantics, alternative text, measured contrast, errors,
  validation, unavailable, success, and reduced-motion behavior

## Gate Decision

**Major Revision.** Proceed to one bounded v3 revision and repeat Design Artifact
Review. Do not proceed to stakeholder approval or Vendor Handoff. Preserve the
improved package-governance structure, route separation, page role, and journey.
If v3 requires product or operating decisions rather than placeholder
corrections, return those issues to Design Review or the responsible owner.
