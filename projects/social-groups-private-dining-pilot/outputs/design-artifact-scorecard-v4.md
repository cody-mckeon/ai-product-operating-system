## Blocking And Major Findings

- **Major:** No mobile v4 evidence was supplied.
- **Major:** Stage 7 inquiry content is visually unreadable at stakeholder review
  size.
- **Major:** Stage 7 asserts that downstream purposes must not share a form,
  route, owner, or confirmation behavior, resolving an open operating decision.

## Minor Findings

- Planning Support and Planning Guide use directional examples that are too
  specific for unconfirmed capability and qualification scope.
- Team/Proof and Packages remain more governance-led than guest-experience-led.
- RWLV brand and existing global page context are under-evidenced.
- Annotation placement, contrast, and visual priority are inconsistent.

## Verification Gaps

- Complete desktop page continuity, transitions, total length, and relative
  stage emphasis.
- Mobile hierarchy, reflow, annotation placement, action clarity, and touch
  behavior.
- Existing locked RWLV header/footer or preserved global context.
- Figma editability, component structure, and interaction states.
- Image source, rights, crop approval, freshness, and replacement status.
- Team, capability, package, proof, CTA, and routing approvals.
- Technical accessibility behavior and measured compliance.

## Gate Decision

**Major Revision.** The concept is strategically intact and can be corrected
through a bounded v5 revision. It is not ready for stakeholder review or Vendor
Handoff as supplied. Revise the inquiry stage, restore route neutrality,
provide complete desktop/mobile evidence, and address the bounded minor findings
before another Design Artifact Review.
