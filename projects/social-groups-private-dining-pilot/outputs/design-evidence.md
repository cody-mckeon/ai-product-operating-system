# Social Groups / Private Dining — Design Evidence

## Evidence Basis

This synthesis uses 35 desktop screenshots, `outputs/current-state.md`, and six supplied design-system documents. A pattern is treated as recurring when it appears in at least two relevant screenshot examples or is explicitly named in current design-system documentation. One-off structures are listed separately.

Evidence labels are **Observed**, **Documented**, and **Inferred**. Confidence refers to the certainty of the visual pattern, not its suitability for a future solution.

## Hospitality Behaviors

- **Observed — visual orientation before detail:** full-width or prominent environmental photography precedes copy and operational content across Meetings, Group Dining, Team, Zouk, and FUHU.
- **Observed — service made human in two different ways:** RWLV shows a host/presenter in context and organized contact access; Zouk shows named staff, portraits, and biographies.
- **Observed — practical reassurance after atmosphere:** FUHU moves from venue mood to capacities and floor plan; Zouk moves from nightlife imagery to specs; RWLV Event Venues pairs room imagery with size, description, and spec/tour actions.
- **Observed — sense of place:** photographs retain recognizable restaurant, ballroom, nightlife, patio, lectern, and property details rather than relying on abstract luxury imagery.
- **Documented:** `ai-design-kit.md` and `visual-principles.md` describe orientation, clarity, restraint, and conversion as hospitality. The screenshot sequence is consistent with that posture in the reviewed desktop states.

## Discovery Behaviors

- RWLV Meetings uses an anchor bar to expose Event Venues, Unique Venues, Group Dining, and Concierge near the top of the page.
- RWLV Event Venues uses a vertical named selector and one selected detail panel.
- RWLV Group Dining exposes experience settings through separate editorial chapters rather than a repeated venue grid.
- Zouk Private Sales first shows five venue/logo images, then expands venues into long-form image/specification chapters.
- FUHU offers adjacent Zouk venue links beneath the hero but keeps the body focused on one venue.
- The RFP uses category tabs for task selection rather than discovery imagery.

## Conversion Behaviors

- RWLV editorial pages use red outlined actions after explanatory copy; a solid red subnav action remains visible at the category level.
- Zouk repeats `REQUEST PROPOSAL` at multiple journey depths while retaining a consistent black-outline control on light surfaces.
- FUHU varies CTA fill according to background: white on dark hero imagery and charcoal on the light floor-plan surface.
- The RFP removes imagery and concentrates attention on category selection, labeled fields, required indicators, and submit.
- Current-state evidence records separate destinations and forms; the screenshots establish visual treatment, not back-end routing.

## Reusable Experience Structures

### Image-led arrival with immediate orientation

- **Observed purpose:** establish place and page category.
- **Sequence:** environmental image → category/title → short context → optional action or anchor navigation.
- **Placement:** page opening.
- **Variations:** RWLV title below image; Zouk/FUHU title over or directly above image.
- **Evidence:** Meetings hero/anchor, Group Dining hero/intro, Zouk hero, FUHU hero.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Editorial category chapter

- **Observed purpose:** explain a portfolio area without flattening it into a generic card.
- **Sequence:** eyebrow → title → copy → image → context-specific action.
- **Placement:** mid-page discovery.
- **Variations:** split, overlap, full-bleed image with inset panel, layered imagery.
- **Evidence:** RWLV Meetings and Group Dining screenshots.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Team identity chapter

- **Observed purpose:** make service ownership visible.
- **Sequence:** collective promise → human evidence → access or biography.
- **Variations:** RWLV contextual collective; Zouk named individual.
- **Evidence:** RWLV Team and Zouk Meet the Team.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Portfolio preview to detail

- **Observed purpose:** make breadth scannable before exposing dense specifications.
- **Sequence:** venue/category names or images → selected/expanded detail → image/spec proof → action.
- **Evidence:** RWLV Event Venues; Zouk intro and venue catalog.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Dedicated inquiry environment

- **Observed purpose:** transition from editorial browsing to structured request capture.
- **Sequence:** category selector → form title → grouped questions → submit.
- **Evidence:** RWLV RFP screenshots; Zouk overlay behavior is documented but not visually supplied.
- **Confidence:** High for RWLV, Low for Zouk visual treatment.

## Contradictions And Unresolved Evidence

- `zouk-private-sales/08-request-proposal-overlay.png` does not show an overlay, while `outputs/current-state.md` confirms a live overlay was observed. The overlay's visual design remains unverified in the screenshot set.
- RWLV RFP submit controls appear solid red in Group Dining and outlined red in Weddings/Celebrations. The evidence does not establish whether this is a state, template, or capture difference.
- Zouk Private Sales uses softly rounded outlined controls; FUHU uses pill-shaped filled controls. No single Zouk-wide CTA geometry can be generalized.
- RWLV and Zouk use materially different heading weight, color, navigation, and proof languages. The evidence supports coexistence, not one universal styling rule.
- Capture dates and mobile behavior are unknown.
