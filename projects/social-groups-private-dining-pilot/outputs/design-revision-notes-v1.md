## Content And Proof Guardrails

- Treat all v1 consumer copy as exploratory unless an upstream source explicitly
  confirms it for publication.
- Do not invent or approve team members, titles, biographies, collective scope,
  ownership, handoffs, response expectations, or specialist relationships.
- Do not treat generic people imagery, crowds, property images, venue marks, or
  atmosphere as proof of actual service capability.
- Do not use testimonials, client marks, event results, publication evidence,
  venue facts, capacities, or operational statements without source, rights,
  approval, freshness, and relevance.
- Do not convert the pilot's in-scope list into verified services, availability,
  bundles, or fulfillment promises.
- Do not invent package names, audiences, value, benefits, inclusions,
  exclusions, pricing, terms, eligibility, dates, inventory, availability,
  imagery, proof, owner, action, or destination.
- Treat August 4 year and meaning, fall, F1, NFR, holidays, weddings, and
  engagement windows as unresolved. Do not imply active seasonal availability.
- Keep package interest and custom-planning interest distinct.
- Do not assume one universal form, route, CRM, owner, data persistence,
  automated personalization, response time, success message, or handoff.
- Do not change existing booking, reservation, Concierge, phone, email, chat,
  RFP, or Zouk proposal behavior.
- Keep internal governance annotations visibly separate from any hypothetical
  public composition.

## Do Not Change

- Do not replace the selected CON-01 direction.
- Do not reorder the approved journey responsibilities.
- Do not move packages or inquiry ahead of orientation and occasion/need
  recognition.
- Do not turn the revision into a venue directory, service catalog, package
  campaign, occasion-card wall, itinerary, long RFP, or Zouk microsite.
- Do not remove the route-neutral distinction among planning, booking,
  reservation, and support.
- Do not populate placeholders merely to make the revision look complete.
- Do not reduce whitespace and chapter pacing simply to fit more portfolio
  items.
- Do not copy Zouk typography, CTA geometry, charcoal page identity, nightlife
  density, or proof treatment as the RWLV visual system.
- Do not redesign the RWLV global website, navigation, footer, RFP, booking
  engine, reservations, or downstream pages.

## Open Decisions

- **One-team scope:** Which organization or role owns the relationship, which
  specialists participate, and what continuity or handoff language is factual?
- **Portfolio taxonomy:** Which occasion/need labels and relationships are
  approved and prioritized, and how do practical needs enter?
- **Capability truth:** Which services are available, to whom, under what
  constraints, and through which owner?
- **Proof governance:** Which people, roles, images, events, marks, facts, and
  results may be used across RWLV and Zouk contexts?
- **Package readiness:** What are the package facts, August 4 year and meaning,
  active/inactive rules, imagery, proof, owner, and route?
- **Seasonal governance:** Who activates and retires fall, F1, NFR, holiday,
  wedding, and engagement content, and what facts support it?
- **Inquiry model:** Is the guest-facing entry shared intake, category
  branching, manual triage, or transparent handoff, and what information,
  privacy behavior, fallback, and response expectation apply?
- **Global frame:** Which existing RWLV header, subnavigation, footer, and
  destination conventions should be shown in this page study?
- **Brand approval:** Which typography, colors, imagery, and cross-brand
  attribution are approved for this RWLV-framed page?

These decisions must remain pending in v2 unless their owners provide approved
evidence. Figma Make must not resolve them by inference.
