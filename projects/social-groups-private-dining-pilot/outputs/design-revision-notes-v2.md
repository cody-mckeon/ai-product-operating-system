## Content And Proof Guardrails

- Treat all v2 headlines, body copy, labels, category names, service
  descriptions, and CTAs as exploratory unless an upstream owner explicitly
  approves them.
- Do not invent a Social Groups Planning team, specialist model, department,
  role, ownership relationship, cross-property coordination promise, or
  continuity behavior.
- Do not convert the pilot's in-scope portfolio into confirmed services,
  availability, qualification logic, or packages.
- Do not invent or approve team members, roles, biographies, testimonials,
  client marks, event outcomes, venue facts, operational facts, or performance
  claims.
- Do not treat generic people, dining, resort, crowd, spa, suite, or event
  imagery as proof.
- Require source, rights, approval, freshness, relevance, owner, and replacement
  rules for every image and proof item.
- Do not invent package names, audience, benefits, inclusions, exclusions,
  pricing, terms, eligibility, inventory, availability, images, proof, owner,
  action, route, or seasonal status.
- Treat August 4, fall, F1, NFR, holidays, weddings, and engagement windows as
  unresolved until dates, usage rights, operational facts, and governance are
  approved.
- Do not assume a universal form, right specialist, automated route, CRM,
  saved state, personalization, response time, confirmation, or handoff.
- Do not redesign existing RWLV navigation, booking, reservations, RFP,
  Concierge, contact, support, footer, or Zouk proposal systems.
- Keep internal governance notes visibly separate from hypothetical consumer
  content.

## Do Not Change

- Do not replace CON-01 or reopen the approved page role.
- Do not reorder the seven journey responsibilities.
- Do not move packages or inquiry before orientation and occasion/need
  discovery.
- Do not restore the v1 package-card grid, Bond Street mark, or invented global
  controls.
- Do not turn the page into a venue directory, service catalog, package
  campaign, fixed itinerary, long form, Zouk clone, or Meetings & Events reskin.
- Do not collapse hotel reservations, table reservations, property support, and
  planning inquiry into one action.
- Do not populate placeholders to make v3 look production-ready.
- Do not reduce the generous pacing solely to display more portfolio items.
