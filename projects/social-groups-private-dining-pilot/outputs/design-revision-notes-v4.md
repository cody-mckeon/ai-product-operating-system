## Content And Proof Guardrails

- Treat all v4 copy and CTA labels as directional draft material, not approved
  production content.
- Do not invent or confirm a Social Groups Planning team, department,
  specialist model, owner, handoff, coordination promise, or universal
  coverage.
- Do not turn the pilot's portfolio into confirmed services, bundles,
  capabilities, availability, package content, or qualification rules.
- Do not invent team members, titles, biographies, testimonials, clients,
  marks, quotes, event examples, outcomes, awards, capacities, venue facts,
  operational facts, or performance claims.
- Do not use generic people, dining, resort, spa, suite, or event imagery as
  proof.
- Require source, rights, approval, freshness, relevance, owner, accessibility,
  and replacement status for every future proof or image asset.
- Do not invent package names, audiences, value, benefits, inclusions,
  exclusions, pricing, terms, eligibility, inventory, availability, dates,
  imagery, proof, owner, action, route, or seasonal status.
- Treat August 4, fall, F1, NFR, holidays, weddings, and engagement windows as
  unresolved until timing, rights, facts, owners, activation, and retirement
  rules are approved.
- Do not assume or prohibit one form, shared intake, category branching, manual
  triage, automated routing, CRM, saved state, personalization, owner, response
  time, confirmation, or handoff.
- Keep directional actions nonfunctional and visibly annotated until labels,
  destinations, and behavior are approved.
- Do not redesign existing RWLV navigation, booking, reservation, RFP,
  Concierge, contact, support, footer, or Zouk proposal systems.

## Do Not Change

- Do not replace CON-01 or reopen the approved page role.
- Do not reorder, remove, or merge the seven stages.
- Do not move packages or inquiry before orientation and occasion/need
  recognition.
- Do not return to a venue directory, service catalog, package campaign,
  six-card occasion wall, fixed itinerary, long form, Zouk clone, Meetings &
  Events reskin, or internal department page.
- Do not restore the v1 package-card grid, Bond Street mark, or invented global
  controls.
- Do not populate proof or packages to make the concept appear complete.
- Do not convert all unresolved content back into bracketed placeholders.
- Do not remove annotations merely to make the concept look more polished.
- Do not collapse planning inquiry, hotel reservations, table reservations, and
  property support into one undifferentiated action.
- Do not add nightlife dominance, casino clichés, neon/glow excess, urgency,
  dense grids, or generic luxury styling.
