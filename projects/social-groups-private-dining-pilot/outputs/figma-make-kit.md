# Social Groups / Private Dining — Figma Make Kit

## Scope And Evidence

The kit derives from 35 desktop screenshots across RWLV Meetings & Events, Group Dining, RFP, Our Team, Zouk Private Sales, and FUHU Private Events. It describes visual grammar only. It does not define a landing-page concept, content order, final design, or implementation.

## Visual Language

- **Observed foundation:** RWLV combines cinematic property photography with white/pale surfaces, restrained red signals, editorial typography, generous space, and functional inquiry states. Zouk combines neutral UI surfaces with saturated nightlife/event imagery, heavy headings, human proof, and detailed venue chapters.
- **Inferred guidance:** maintain a balance of immersive, editorial, informational, and transactional content. Let each section have one visual lead and one clear content role.
- **Evidence:** `rwlv-meetings-events/*`, `rwlv-group-dining/*`, `zouk-private-sales/*`, `fuhu-private-events/*`.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Typography Guidance

- Preserve qualitative role contrast: display, category eyebrow, section heading, body, operational label, navigation/tab, and CTA.
- In RWLV context, use the observed thin uppercase display posture with small red uppercase eyebrows and compact dark body copy.
- In Zouk-derived context, distinguish thin arrival statements from heavy uppercase team, proof, and venue headings.
- Use functional, scannable typography for forms and specifications.
- Do not invent exact font names, sizes, weights, tracking, or line heights.

## Color Usage Guidance

- Treat white and pale neutral surfaces as the primary content field.
- Treat RWLV red as an action, category, required-field, and active-state signal rather than an all-surface fill.
- Treat the warm contour motif as an observed RWLV supporting texture, not a universal background requirement.
- Let venue/event photography supply localized atmosphere and color.
- Keep Zouk nightlife color bound to relevant imagery; do not convert it into an unverified property-wide palette.
- Do not invent color values.

## CTA Guidance

- Keep CTA language explicit and uppercase where it matches the observed family: connect, request proposal, book event, reserve, learn, filter, submit.
- Place actions after enough identity or detail is visible to explain the intent.
- Preserve family differences: RWLV red outline/text/filled treatments; Zouk black-outline controls; FUHU contrast-dependent pill controls.
- Use paired actions only when their intents are visibly distinct, as in RWLV Social Groups Concierge or Event Venues.
- Do not infer hover, focus, disabled, validation, or success styling from static evidence.

## Card And Panel Guidance

- Use cards selectively by content role rather than as a default container.
- Evidence-backed structures include: RWLV master/detail venue panel, overlapping editorial copy panel, thin-bordered contact cards, Zouk venue image row, large proof field, and long-form specification chapter.
- Preserve information order when represented: category/name → image or person → description → evidence/specification → action.
- Avoid nested cards and equal-weight grids unsupported by the screenshot set.

## Team-Led Visual Grammar

- **RWLV observed model:** contextual service image, collective destination-expert message, connect action, and functional contact methods.
- **Zouk observed model:** department promise, named portrait, role/biography, then client/event proof.
- **Inferred grammar:** team identity can be expressed through human presence, explicit roles, service evidence, and reachable contact paths.
- Do not invent staff, titles, biographies, portraits, or combined-team claims.

## Portfolio-Led Visual Grammar

- **RWLV observed model:** anchor navigation, category chapters, and master/detail venue selection.
- **Zouk observed model:** compact venue/logo image row followed by immersive venue chapters and specifications.
- **FUHU observed model:** one venue explored through atmosphere, rooms, capacities, patio, and floor plan.
- **Inferred grammar:** establish portfolio relationships before dense detail; retain distinct venue identities and contextual evidence.
- Do not turn venue logos into unexplained proof or invent participation.

## Inquiry-Led Visual Grammar

- **RWLV observed model:** a dedicated white form environment with framed category tabs, centered title, grouped two-column fields, red required markers, and submit action.
- **Zouk observed behavior:** repeated proposal actions and a live overlay recorded in current state; overlay styling is not visually verified by the supplied screenshots.
- **Inferred grammar:** distinguish inquiry from inspiration through simpler surfaces, explicit classification, and organized operational questions.
- Do not invent form rules, destinations, required fields, routing, or completion states.

## Imagery Guidance

- Use specific, legible environment and experience evidence: venue arrival, room setup, private space, patio, bar, ballroom, social moment, event, staff, or floor plan.
- Match image energy to content role: calm architectural clarity for evaluation; social/event energy for proof; human service context for team framing.
- Preserve environmental cues and recognizable venue identity.
- Use text over imagery only where contrast and subject clarity are visibly protected, as in the FUHU hero and Zouk Client Spotlights.
- Do not invent imagery for packages or occasions absent from the evidence set.

## Page Rhythm Guidance

- Alternate lower-density arrival/orientation with moderate-density discovery and denser proof/specification/inquiry states.
- Vary editorial composition across chapters: centered orientation, split image/text, overlap, layered image, selector/detail, proof field, and form.
- Keep operational detail visually grouped with the venue or inquiry it describes.
- Use whitespace to separate roles and chapters, not to imply unverified importance.
- This cadence is a grammar, not a prescribed module order.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic luxury styling with no traceable RWLV or Zouk evidence.
- Generic SaaS/dashboard conventions.
- Unverified fonts, colors, gradients, glows, tokens, spacing, motion, or component dimensions.
- Repetitive equal-weight card walls replacing the observed editorial variety.
- Nightlife neon applied indiscriminately outside relevant Zouk imagery.
- Testimonial, logo, or partner decoration without confirmed source and governance.
- Invented amenities, packages, prices, benefits, capacities, events, team members, or interactions.
- Treating the mislabeled Zouk screenshot as proof of overlay design.
- Page concepts that erase the distinction between property-wide RWLV context, venue-specific Zouk context, and task-specific inquiry context.

## Reusable Guidance Block

> **Observed foundation:** RWLV uses cinematic property imagery, white and pale warm surfaces, restrained red action/category signals, a subtle contour motif, thin uppercase display typography, varied editorial modules, clear venue detail, and a separate functional RFP state. Zouk Private Sales uses a black/white/gray interface, saturated venue imagery, heavy uppercase section headings, named-person team proof, client/event proof, venue recognition imagery, detailed specifications, and repeated proposal actions. FUHU uses venue-led hero, image/spec alternation, floor-plan proof, and contrast-dependent pill CTAs.
>
> **Inferred guidance:** preserve one focal point per section; distinguish arrival, discovery, proof/detail, and inquiry; use people and operational evidence to make service tangible; explain portfolio relationships before dense detail; keep actions explicit and visually subordinate to understanding until the inquiry state.
>
> **Do not invent:** fonts, token values, colors, dimensions, responsive rules, team members, testimonials, client marks, package content, venue facts, imagery, form behavior, or overlay styling.
>
> **Scope limitations:** desktop screenshots only; capture dates, mobile states, interaction states, asset rights, and several stakeholder-requested portfolio areas are unverified.
