# Figma Prompts

These prompts translate CON-01 and approved directions DR-01 through DR-05 into one primary page exploration and four supporting explorations. No project-level `component-inventory.md` was available, so component guidance is limited to documented RWLV pattern families and observed current-page behaviors; reuse must be verified before production design.

## Prompt 1 — Primary Landing Page Exploration

### Purpose

Explore the complete page-level expression of DR-01 — Unified Planning Proposition, using DR-02 — Occasion / Need Discovery Layer as the primary discovery model and keeping DR-03, DR-04, and DR-05 visibly conditional.

### Context to provide Figma Make

- **Design direction ID:** DR-01, supported by DR-02, DR-03, DR-04, and DR-05.
- **Surface type:** Standalone Page.
- **Existing surface:** A new dedicated Social Groups / Private Dining landing page inside the existing RWLV website and property frame.
- **Existing components to preserve:** RWLV global navigation, footer, property identity, established page context, and existing downstream discovery or inquiry destinations. Do not redesign those systems.
- **Recommended placement:** A primary property-wide orientation and inquiry entry linked from relevant RWLV and approved Zouk discovery surfaces.
- **Module scope:** Large; multiple connected modules forming one landing-page journey.
- **Core experience pattern:** One continuous planning relationship followed by occasion/need discovery, evidence, current relevance, qualification context, and truthful inquiry.
- **Reuse basis:** Experience Hero, Editorial Feature, Content Proof Module, Venue Orientation Feature, CTA Block, and optionally Page Anchor Navigation. Reuse is conceptual until a component inventory confirms production components.

### Prompt text

```text
Create an exploratory landing-page direction in Figma Make for Resorts World Las Vegas Social Groups / Private Dining. This is an exploratory design study, not final production design and not final copy.

CONTEXT
The approved concept is CON-01 — One Team, Many Occasions. The primary direction is DR-01 — Unified Planning Proposition, with DR-02 — Occasion / Need Discovery Layer as the discovery model. Conditional supporting layers are DR-03 — Team And Proof Credibility, DR-04 — Packages In Context, and DR-05 — Guided Inquiry.

The page must communicate one continuous planning relationship supported by verified specialists across the property. Do not imply that one individual, one department, one form, one CRM route, or one operating system directly fulfills every service.

EXPERIENCE INTEGRATION CONTEXT
Surface Type: Standalone Page
Existing Surface: A new dedicated Social Groups / Private Dining page within the existing RWLV website
Existing Components To Preserve: RWLV global navigation, footer, property identity, established site conventions, and current downstream pages or inquiry destinations
Recommended Placement: Primary property-wide orientation and inquiry entry for social planning

Guests may know their occasion or practical need but not the correct venue or department.

Use the entire page as the orientation and inquiry entry. Preserve deeper venue, package, resource, booking, reservation, and operational form destinations as downstream experiences rather than recreating them here.

2. Guest occasion or planning need
3. Confirmed capabilities and RWLV property breadth
4. Approved team, event, property, and operational proof

6. Qualification context and truthful inquiry

Treat this as a journey hierarchy, not six equal visual sections. The one-team proposition and occasion/need discovery are primary. Services, venues, packages, proof, and seasons are supporting evidence or current context.

Use documented RWLV pattern families where appropriate: Experience Hero, Editorial Feature, Content Proof Module, Venue Orientation Feature, CTA Block, and optional Page Anchor Navigation. Treat Social Proof Layer, Package Card, Offer Card, Benefit Validation Module, and FAQ / Support Continuity as conditional because their content is unresolved.

Use real-image placeholders labeled by required asset role, such as [APPROVED RWLV SOCIAL OCCASION IMAGE], [APPROVED DINING OR PRIVATE-EVENT PROOF], and [APPROVED STAY / HOSPITALITY IMAGE]. Do not generate fake proof imagery or present placeholders as approved production assets.

- Governed team and proof layer using clearly labeled placeholders for approved roles, people, events, property evidence, and operational facts.
```

### Required sections or modules

- Transition into planning preparation.
- Minimal qualification-topic placeholders.
- Route-neutral readiness or summary cue.
- One unresolved primary planning action.
- Internal routing annotations.
- Conditional alternate-path hierarchy.
- Placeholder state responsibilities.

### Visual direction

Calm, structured, and hospitable. Use RWLV form clarity without reproducing a long RFP. Avoid a SaaS wizard, dashboard, or pressure-heavy sticky conversion pattern.

### Content placeholders

Use requirement-level placeholders, not realistic form questions or action labels. Mark owner, destination, response, handoff, and state messaging as pending.

### Guardrails

Do not select shared intake, branching, triage, or handoff. Do not portray internal review annotations as consumer choices. Do not imply the guest's context can be saved or routed automatically.

### Mobile considerations

Keep the section short, sequential, keyboard-friendly, and clear about optionality and action purpose. Do not reproduce the density of a desktop RFP.

### What success looks like

The exploration shows how inquiry can feel like a natural continuation of hospitality while remaining operationally honest and compatible with more than one future routing decision.
