## Designer-Facing Notes

### Note 1 — Directional Copy Is Not Approval

- **Note:** Keep the supplied copy at the level of stakeholder-readable
  direction. If a line begins to imply a service, team, package, route, or
  operating fact, generalize it and strengthen the nearby annotation.
- **Why Figma Make alone may not resolve it:** The line between useful concept
  language and an operational claim requires editorial and product judgment.
- **Owner or decision-maker, if known:** RWLV brand, content, product, and
  operations owners
- **Blocking status:** Blocking for production copy approval; non-blocking for
  stakeholder concept review when annotations remain visible.

### Note 2 — Imagery And Rights

- **Note:** Prefer approved RWLV assets. If approval or rights are unavailable,
  use directional imagery only as atmosphere and annotate source, rights, crop,
  relevance, and replacement status. Never use it as proof.
- **Why Figma Make alone may not resolve it:** Rights, freshness, representative
  coverage, brand approval, and cross-brand permissions require human review.
- **Owner or decision-maker, if known:** RWLV brand, content, and legal owners
- **Blocking status:** Blocking for production and proof approval; non-blocking
  for annotated stakeholder concept review.

### Note 3 — Annotation Placement

- **Note:** Manually refine annotations after generation so they remain clearly
  connected to their sections, readable in exports, and secondary to the guest
  experience.
- **Why Figma Make alone may not resolve it:** Automated generation may overlap
  notes, push them into the consumer composition, or reduce them below a
  practical review size.
- **Owner or decision-maker, if known:** Product design owner
- **Blocking status:** Blocking for stakeholder readability.

### Note 4 — Mobile Composition

- **Note:** Review mobile manually to confirm intentional hierarchy, proof
  adjacency, package fallback continuity, action distinction, and annotation
  placement.
- **Why Figma Make alone may not resolve it:** Reading order and annotation
  behavior can degrade when desktop sections are mechanically stacked.
- **Owner or decision-maker, if known:** Product design owner
- **Blocking status:** Blocking for the next Design Artifact Review.

### Note 5 — Existing RWLV Global Components

- **Note:** Use actual header and footer components only when authoritative
  references are available; otherwise use the neutral preserved-context bands.
- **Why Figma Make alone may not resolve it:** Guessing the site frame would
  repeat the v1 global-navigation drift.
- **Owner or decision-maker, if known:** RWLV design-system or web owner
- **Blocking status:** Blocking for production and Vendor Handoff; non-blocking
  for a clearly annotated stakeholder concept.

- Preserve CON-01, the approved page role, and all seven journey stages.
- Resolve the readability gap between v2's false completeness and v3's
  placeholder-dominant governance treatment.
- Keep the main composition stakeholder-readable through directional draft
  copy while keeping unresolved decisions honest through compact annotations.
- Prevent invented package, proof, routing, team, capability, availability, and
  operating facts.
- Apply the RWLV AI Brand Context without inventing production tokens or assets.
- Produce deliberate, inspectable desktop and mobile states.
- Remain an exploratory stakeholder concept rather than a production-ready
  page, internal wireframe, or strategy board.
- Provide evidence suitable for another Design Artifact Review.
