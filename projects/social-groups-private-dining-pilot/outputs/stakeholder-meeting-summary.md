# Social Groups / Private Dining — Stakeholder Meeting Summary

## Purpose Of The Concept

This concept explores a dedicated Resorts World Las Vegas landing page for
Social Groups / Private Dining. The goal is to give offerings that are currently
spread across RWLV, Group Dining, Meetings & Events, Zouk Private Sales, and
separate inquiry paths one clearer place to be understood.

The concept is directional. It is intended to align stakeholders on the page's
role, story, hierarchy, and priorities before final design, copy, and
implementation planning.

## Core Direction

The central idea is **one planning relationship for many social occasions**.

The page would help guests begin with what they are planning, understand the
range of relevant possibilities across the property, build confidence, and
reach an appropriate next step.

This is a proposed guest-facing direction—not confirmation that one team, one
owner, one form, or one route currently supports every request. The operating
model and the language used to describe it still require stakeholder alignment.

## Why This Direction

- Current content and inquiry paths are fragmented across multiple pages,
  brands, categories, and destinations.
- Guests are more likely to begin with an occasion or practical need than with
  knowledge of an internal department.
- RWLV provides the property breadth, hospitality context, and editorial frame
  needed for the broader story.
- Zouk Private Sales provides useful team, portfolio, and proof storytelling
  behaviors, without needing to become the page's visual template.
- Social Groups Hospitality Packages need a governed place within the
  experience without becoming the identity of the entire page.

## Proposed Experience Flow

1. **Arrival and orientation**
2. **Occasion or need recognition**
3. **Property breadth**
4. **Confidence and proof**
5. **Packages and current relevance**
6. **Planning preparation**
7. **Inquiry continuity**

The flow moves from inspiration and orientation toward evidence, preparation,
and action. Packages, proof, and inquiry support the story rather than leading
it.

## What Is Directional

The concept's copy, imagery, occasion taxonomy, capability examples, proof
structure, package presentation, CTA labels, and inquiry routing are all
directional and pending approval.

The design does not approve package details, team ownership, proof, service
availability, final actions, or an inquiry destination. It demonstrates how the
page could work once those inputs are confirmed.

## Decisions Needed From Stakeholders

- What can we truthfully promise as one planning relationship?
- Which offerings belong on the page, and which should be prioritized?
- Which occasions and practical needs should lead the experience?
- What are the approved package details, timing, ownership, and next step?
- What inquiry path, routing model, and ownership structure should be used?
- Which team, event, property, client, and operational proof assets are approved?
- How should RWLV and Zouk be represented together in brand, content, and
  imagery?

## Recommended Next Step

Use the concept first to align on the direction and resolve the decisions above.
After stakeholder alignment, refine the design, directional copy, approved
assets, desktop and mobile experience, package treatment, proof layer, and
inquiry approach.

The work does not need a new strategic concept. It needs stakeholder
validation, approved inputs, and bounded visual refinement.
