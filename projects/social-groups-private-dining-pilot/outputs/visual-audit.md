# Social Groups / Private Dining — Visual Audit

## Title And Scope

This audit documents the visible production language in 35 supplied desktop PNG screenshots covering six page families: RWLV Meetings & Events, RWLV Group Dining, RWLV Request for Proposal, RWLV Our Team, Zouk Private Sales, and FUHU Private Events.

**Evidence date:** screenshot capture dates are not embedded in the supplied evidence. `outputs/current-state.md` records a live-page review on July 6, 2026.

**Device scope:** desktop only. Screenshot widths range from focused component crops to wide page sections; the set does not establish responsive rules.

**Boundary:** this file records observed visual evidence. It does not prescribe a landing-page sequence, select a future treatment, or define a concept.

## Evidence Labels

- **Observed:** directly visible in a supplied screenshot or explicitly recorded in `outputs/current-state.md`.
- **Documented:** stated in a supplied design-system source but not necessarily visible in every screenshot.
- **Unverified:** not confirmable from the available screenshot set.

## Page-Family Visual Summaries

### RWLV Meetings & Events

**Observed, desktop only; high confidence.**

- A full-width banquet-room photograph creates the opening visual field, followed immediately by a slim white `ON THIS PAGE` anchor bar (`rwlv-meetings-events/01-hero.png`, `02-on-this-page-anchor.png`).
- The opening introduction uses centered typography on a white field with a faint warm contour-line motif. A red uppercase eyebrow, large dark display heading, compact centered paragraph, and red outlined CTA form a single vertical stack (`03-intro.png`).
- Event Venues is presented as a large white, lightly elevated rounded panel. Floral venue selectors form a dark image-led stack on the left; selected venue imagery, title, category, size, copy, and filled/outlined actions occupy the right (`04-event-venues.png`).
- Unique Venues uses an asymmetric image-and-card composition. A large venue photograph holds an oversized white headline; a white copy card overlaps its right edge; the warm contour motif fills the lower background (`05-unique-venues.png`).
- Group Dining uses layered imagery: a full-height faded background crop, a smaller crisp foreground image, and a text block with a very wide red outlined CTA (`06-group-dining-section.png`).
- Social Groups Concierge uses a contained two-column split with copy and paired actions on the left and a guest lifestyle image on the right (`07-social-groups-concierge.png`).
- The Meetings & Events subnav is a solid red horizontal band with white uppercase links and a darker-red proposal action (`08-subnav.png`).

### RWLV Group Dining

**Observed, desktop only; high confidence.**

- A full-width private-dining-room image is followed by a centered introduction on the contour-pattern surface. The hierarchy is eyebrow, title, short copy, and outlined action (`rwlv-group-dining/01-hero-intro-book-event.png`).
- Private Dining Rooms uses a contained side-by-side editorial composition: image left, red eyebrow and large dark heading right, with generous white space around both (`02-private-dining-rooms.png`).
- Outdoor Dining places a large white rounded copy panel over the lower portion of a full-width environmental image. Copy and action are centered (`03-outdoor-dining.png`).
- Bars & Lounges reverses the split: copy left, image right, with the contour motif cropped as a large circular background accent behind the image. The action appears as a red text link with arrow (`04-bars-lounges.png`).
- The closing Group Dining Options block is centered and image-free, using a title, compact paragraph, and outlined button (`05-group-dining-options.png`).
- The page family alternates full-bleed imagery, contained editorial splits, an overlapping panel, and a centered closing action instead of repeating one card format.

### RWLV Request For Proposal

**Observed, desktop only; high confidence.**

- The RFP is predominantly white, typographic, and form-led. The global RWLV header and red action treatment remain visible (`rwlv-rfp/01-rfp-page-default.png`, `03-group-dining-form.png`, `04-weddings-form.png`, `05-celebrations-form.png`).
- A thin black rectangular tab container presents four uppercase categories. The selected tab is marked by darker/bolder text and a short red underline (`02-rfp-tabs.png`).
- Forms use thin gray input borders, small black labels, red required markers, and large two-column desktop groupings. Section headings are uppercase and left aligned.
- Page/form titles are centered, thin-weight, uppercase display text. Weddings and Celebrations use hospitable verb-led titles: `LET'S PLAN YOUR WEDDING` and `LET'S PLAN YOUR CELEBRATION`.
- Submit treatments vary in the captures: Group Dining shows a compact solid-red button; Weddings and Celebrations show red outlined buttons (`03-group-dining-form.png`, `04-weddings-form.png`, `05-celebrations-form.png`).
- Image use is absent in the form body; operational clarity, grouping, and whitespace carry the hierarchy.

### RWLV Our Team

**Observed, desktop only; high confidence.**

- The hero is a large contextual hospitality image of a person presenting at an RWLV lectern to a seated audience, rather than a named staff portrait (`rwlv-team/01-team-hero.png`).

## Color Usage Observations

- RWLV pages use white and pale warm-gray fields as the dominant interface surfaces, with charcoal text and red for category labels, active states, required markers, borders, and actions.
- A faint warm beige contour-line motif appears behind RWLV arrival and editorial sections, creating a property-specific supporting texture without replacing photography.
- RWLV venue imagery supplies most local color: warm wood, amber dining light, green/black nightlife, blue ballroom carpet, and guest wardrobe.
- Zouk Private Sales uses a neutral interface of white, pale gray, charcoal, and black while saturated venue photography introduces purple, blue, green, and red nightlife color.
- Zouk proof and past-event modules use large charcoal/gray blocks as compositional fields.
- FUHU remains mostly white/light gray, with olive, pink, orange, and wood tones supplied by venue photography; its CTAs use black/charcoal and white rather than RWLV red.
- Exact color values are unverified. The design-system sources confirm red as an RWLV brand/action signal but do not provide reusable values in the supplied files.

## Card Observations

- RWLV does not use one universal card. The supplied evidence includes a large rounded master/detail venue panel, an overlapping white editorial panel, and thin-bordered utility contact cards (`rwlv-meetings-events/04-event-venues.png`, `05-unique-venues.png`; `rwlv-team/02-contact-options.png`).
- The Event Venues panel contains venue selectors, selected imagery, venue/category labels, scale, description, and paired actions in one elevated surface.
- RWLV Team contact cards contain a line icon, bold functional label, and underlined destination; they have thin borders and no visible image crop or shadow.
- Zouk's venue image row functions as a portfolio selector using darkened photography and venue logos, but the items are visually flush and image-led rather than presented as conventional bordered cards (`zouk-private-sales/02-private-sales-intro.png`).
- Zouk's team, proof, and venue details are large chapter-like fields rather than small repeated cards.
- The RFP uses simple fields and a framed tab row without card nesting.
- Mobile card stacking, horizontal scrolling, selected states, and responsive image crops are unverified.

## Inquiry Pattern Observations

- RWLV visually separates inspiration from inquiry: editorial and team pages use red CTAs, while the destination RFP becomes an image-free, form-led environment with category tabs and grouped inputs.
- The RFP tab row gives equal visual access to Meetings & Events, Group Dining, Weddings, and Celebrations; the active category is indicated with a short red underline.
- Zouk repeats the same proposal label across hero, collective introduction, and venue-detail areas. Its outlined black control remains visually consistent across light and image-led contexts.
- FUHU repeats proposal access at the hero and floor-plan end state but changes from a white pill to a dark pill according to background contrast.
- The live Zouk overlay structure is documented in `outputs/current-state.md`; its visual overlay treatment is unverified by the screenshot labeled as an overlay.

## Imagery Observations

- RWLV Meetings and Group Dining favor clear establishing views of real spaces: banquet configurations, private dining rooms, patios, bars, ballrooms, and nightlife venues.
- Human presence is selective. RWLV Team and Social Groups Concierge show active hospitality/social moments; most venue images are unoccupied or lightly populated to clarify the space.
- Zouk Private Sales uses higher-energy nightlife color, event crowds, lighting effects, named venue logos, client-event photography, and a cutout staff portrait.
- Zouk Client Spotlights places readable white copy over a dark, relatively low-detail area of event imagery.
- FUHU images move from atmospheric arrival to detailed proof: venue entry, dining room, private room, patio, then floor plan.
- No package, room-block, in-suite hospitality, F1, NFR, wedding ceremony, bachelorette, or engagement-shoot imagery appears in the supplied screenshot set.

## Spacing Observations

- RWLV editorial modules use broad outer margins, centered or two-column containers, and generous vertical space between eyebrow, heading, copy, and action.
- RWLV RFP condenses spacing within field groups while retaining large breaks between form sections and a wide centered form column.
- Zouk Private Sales moves between dense immersive imagery and very open pale-gray/white content fields. Team and venue sections use large horizontal spans and substantial whitespace around bold headings.
- FUHU uses wide whitespace and a slow alternating image/text rhythm; operational content remains grouped close to its associated image.
- Exact spacing values and container widths are unverified.

## Hierarchy Observations

- RWLV hierarchy generally proceeds from image-led arrival to category label, display heading, concise explanation, and action.
- Zouk hierarchy proceeds from high-energy arrival to collective capability, venue recognition, team identity, client/event proof, and detailed venue specifications.
- FUHU hierarchy proceeds from venue arrival to narrative orientation, operational specifications, private-space detail, and floor plan.
- RFP hierarchy is task-led: category tabs, form title, section title, field groups, submit.
- In all families, one dominant visual or heading leads each captured section; supporting copy and action occupy a clearly secondary scale.

## Page Rhythm Observations

- RWLV Meetings and Group Dining alternate full-width photography, centered orientation, asymmetrical editorial splits, layered image treatments, and contained action blocks.
- Zouk alternates immersive venue color with neutral content surfaces, then introduces people, proof, event imagery, and detailed specifications in distinct large chapters.
- FUHU uses a slower venue-documentation cadence: hero, introduction, image/spec split, alternating room/patio sections, floor plan, inquiry.
- The RFP intentionally breaks from the editorial rhythm and uses a sustained functional form surface.
