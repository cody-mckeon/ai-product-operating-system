# Pilot Overview

This pilot begins a manual AI Product Operating System run focused on the potential consolidation of Resorts World Las Vegas Social Groups and Private Dining content. It will establish the evidence base and decision context needed to evaluate how the relevant business areas could be represented through a cohesive landing-page experience.

**Business question:** How should Resorts World Las Vegas consolidate Social Groups, Group Dining, Zouk Private Events, Weddings, celebrations, in-suite hospitality, wellness, bachelorette, and engagement-related experiences into one cohesive landing page that presents one unified team and one clear inquiry path?

# Stakeholder Request

> We need to begin thinking about consolidating all Social Groups / Private Dining business into a single, cohesive landing page.

The stakeholder wants the work on the roadmap soon, with particular attention to the fall social-event season, F1 and NFR hospitality opportunities, and the August 4 launch of new Social Groups Hospitality Packages. The stakeholder referenced the recently redesigned Zouk Private Events experience as an aesthetic point of reference.

# Business Context

- Fall is a peak period for engagements, weddings, holiday parties, and other social events.
- F1 and NFR create opportunities for in-suite food and beverage hospitality business.
- New Social Groups Hospitality Packages are scheduled to launch on August 4 and require dedicated space.
- Group Dining and Zouk Private Events are intended to be represented as one unified department.
- The desired story is broader than any individual venue or offering: one team can coordinate social experiences across the property.
- Existing evidence spans Zouk Group Las Vegas and Resorts World Las Vegas pages, including private events, private sales, meetings and events, group dining, resources, inquiry, team, and property-map content.

# Problem Statement

The Social Groups / Private Dining portfolio spans multiple experience types, customer occasions, operational areas, and existing web pages. The pilot must establish how this current landscape, its audiences, its source content, and its inquiry paths relate before any recommendation is made about a consolidated landing page. The central framing challenge is to preserve the breadth of the portfolio while evaluating the stakeholder's intent to present a unified team and a clear inquiry path.

# Goals

- Document the stakeholder request, business drivers, timing pressures, and portfolio scope.
- Establish the known source-page set for the manual pilot run.
- Identify known audiences and content needs without presuming a final information architecture or design.
- Capture uncertainties that require validation during later evidence gathering.
- Provide a bounded input set for subsequent current-state analysis.

# Initial Hypotheses

These are assumptions to validate during evidence gathering, not recommendations or concepts.

- The current customer journey may distribute related social-event information across multiple pages, brands, and inquiry paths.
- Prospective customers may think first in terms of their occasion or planning need rather than the internal department responsible for fulfillment.
- Some portfolio areas may share audiences, planning requirements, or operational handoffs even when they are presented separately today.
- The unified-team framing may require evidence about cross-property planning capabilities, team ownership, and inquiry routing.
- The August 4 packages and the fall, F1, and NFR demand windows may create different content-readiness and timing requirements.
- The known URLs may not contain all content required to represent room blocks, in-suite hospitality, weddings, wellness, bachelorette experiences, or the Athena engagement photo shoot.
- Existing forms and calls to action may reveal different qualification requirements across portfolio areas.
- The Zouk Private Events reference may provide useful current-state aesthetic evidence, but the specific qualities valued by the stakeholder remain unverified.
