# Company Definition

## Brand

Slip 18

## Positioning line

Your AI Transformation Partner

## Category

AI transformation and agent implementation partner for hospitality organizations.

## Mission

Slip 18 helps hospitality organizations become AI-native by implementing practical AI systems that reduce manual work, improve operational visibility, and help teams execute faster.

## What Slip 18 is not

Slip 18 is not a generic AI chatbot agency.

Slip 18 is not selling AI hype.

Slip 18 is not trying to replace employees.

Slip 18 builds practical workflow systems with humans in control.

## Initial market

Las Vegas and Nevada hospitality first, then broader hospitality organizations.

## Business model direction

Services first, productized services second, potential software platform later.
