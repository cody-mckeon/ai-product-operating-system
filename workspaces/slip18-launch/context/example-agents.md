# Example Agents

## Executive Intelligence Agent

Summarizes important updates across projects, reporting, operations, guest feedback, and leadership priorities.

Primary outcome: leadership visibility without manual status chasing.

## Marketing Intelligence Agent

Summarizes campaign performance, website performance, tracking readiness, and marketing opportunities.

Primary outcome: less manual reporting and faster decision-making.

## Knowledge & SOP Agent

Turns approved documents, policies, training materials, SOPs, FAQs, brand standards, and operating procedures into a searchable AI assistant.

Primary outcome: faster answers and better internal consistency.

## Priority Governor Agent

Reviews tasks, project boards, due dates, blockers, priorities, and ownership to surface what needs attention.

Primary outcome: fewer missed handoffs and clearer execution rhythm.

## Guest Feedback Intelligence Agent

Reviews guest feedback, reviews, survey comments, and recurring complaints to identify themes, risks, and opportunities.

Primary outcome: faster guest-experience insight and action.
