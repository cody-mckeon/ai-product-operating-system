# Sales Principles

## Core rule

Do not sell AI agents. Sell business outcomes.

## Buyer-safe language

Use:

- Reduce manual reporting
- Improve visibility
- Speed up execution
- Turn scattered knowledge into answers
- Give teams leverage
- Start with one workflow

Avoid leading with:

- Autonomous agents
- Replace workers
- Full AI transformation immediately
- Guaranteed ROI
- Unverified savings claims

## First-client sales goal

The first revenue attempt should be a paid Workflow Diagnostic or small Pilot Sprint, not a massive enterprise transformation project.

## First-client target

A local or reachable hospitality operator with:

- A painful workflow
- A reachable decision maker
- Low procurement complexity
- Willingness to test a pilot
