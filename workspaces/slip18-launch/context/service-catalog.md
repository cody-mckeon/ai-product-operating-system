# Service Catalog v0.1

## Offer 1: AI Workflow Diagnostic

### Purpose

Help a hospitality team identify where AI can realistically reduce manual work, improve visibility, or accelerate execution.

### Buyer problem

The organization knows AI matters but does not know where to start.

### Deliverables

- Workflow review
- Stakeholder input
- Current-state friction map
- AI opportunity map
- Prioritized pilot recommendation
- Implementation roadmap

### Initial price target

$2,500 fixed fee for early clients.

## Offer 2: AI Pilot Sprint

### Purpose

Build one working AI-enabled workflow around one high-value use case.

### Buyer problem

A team has a painful recurring workflow and needs a practical implementation, not a strategy deck.

### Deliverables

- Workflow design
- Agent implementation
- Integrations where needed
- Testing and refinement
- Documentation
- Team handoff
- 30 days of support

### Initial price target

$7,500 fixed fee for early clients.

---

## Offer 3: Managed AI Operations

### Purpose

Maintain, improve, and expand AI workflows after launch.

### Buyer problem

AI systems need ongoing care, monitoring, tuning, and governance.

### Deliverables

- Agent monitoring
- Prompt and workflow improvements
- Documentation updates
- Support
- New small enhancements
- Monthly improvement review

### Initial price target

$1,000/month for early clients.
